Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

Which of the following is a constituent of a mixture but not a compound?

  • A. Fixed composition by mass
  • B. Chemical bonds between particles
  • C. Components can be separated by physical means
  • D. New substances are formed
Answer: C. Components can be separated by physical means
Explanation: Mixtures are physical combinations and can be separated by methods like filtration or distillation, unlike compounds which require chemical methods.

Question 2

The electronic configuration of an atom is 2, 8, 7. How many valence electrons does it have?

  • A. 2
  • B. 8
  • C. 7
  • D. 17
Answer: C. 7
Explanation: Valence electrons are the electrons in the outermost shell.

Question 3

Which of the following elements belongs to the same group as Magnesium (Atomic number 12)?

  • A. Sodium (11)
  • B. Calcium (20)
  • C. Aluminum (13)
  • D. Potassium (19)
Answer: B. Calcium (20)
Explanation: Both Magnesium (2, 8, 2) and Calcium (2, 8, 8, 2) have 2 valence electrons and belong to Group 2.

Question 4

The type of bond formed between two atoms of Hydrogen to form H2 is:

  • A. Ionic bond
  • B. Covalent bond
  • C. Metallic bond
  • D. Coordinate bond
Answer: B. Covalent bond
Explanation: Two non-metal atoms of the same element share electrons to form a covalent bond.

Question 5

According to Dalton's atomic theory, atoms are:

  • A. Indivisible and indestructible
  • B. Made of protons and neutrons
  • C. Large heavy particles
  • D. Mostly empty space
Answer: A. Indivisible and indestructible
Explanation: While later proven wrong by the discovery of subatomic particles, this was a primary postulate of Dalton's theory.

Question 6

The state of matter with a fixed volume but no fixed shape is:

  • A. Solid
  • B. Liquid
  • C. Gas
  • D. Plasma
Answer: B. Liquid
Explanation: Liquids take the shape of their container but maintain a constant volume.

Question 7

What is the molar mass of H2O? (H=1, O=16)

  • A. 17 g/mol
  • B. 18 g/mol
  • C. 33 g/mol
  • D. 10 g/mol
Answer: B. 18 g/mol
Explanation: (2 * 1) + 16 = 18.

Question 8

If the pressure of a gas is doubled at constant temperature, the volume will be:

  • A. Doubled
  • B. Halved
  • C. Quadrupled
  • D. Unchanged
Answer: B. Halved
Explanation: According to Boyle's Law, volume is inversely proportional to pressure.

Question 9

Which of the following is an example of a physical property?

  • A. Flammability
  • B. Reactivity with acid
  • C. Boiling point
  • D. Ability to rust
Answer: C. Boiling point
Explanation: Boiling point is observed without changing the substance into a new chemical.

Question 10

The formula for Carbon (II) oxide is:

  • A. CO2
  • B. C2O
  • C. CO
  • D. C2O2
Answer: C. CO
Explanation: Carbon (II) oxide is commonly known as carbon monoxide.

Question 11

An acid that ionizes completely in water is described as a:

  • A. Weak acid
  • B. Strong acid
  • C. Concentrated acid
  • D. Dilute acid
Answer: B. Strong acid
Explanation: Strong acids like HCl or H2SO4 dissociate fully into ions in aqueous solutions.

Question 12

The IUPAC name of CH3CH2OH is:

  • A. Methanol
  • B. Ethanol
  • C. Propanol
  • D. Ethanoic acid
Answer: B. Ethanol
Explanation: Two carbons (eth-) and an alcohol group (-ol).

Question 13

Which of the following gases is used in the manufacture of ammonia?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Nitrogen
  • C. Chlorine
  • D. Argon
Answer: B. Nitrogen
Explanation: Ammonia (NH3) is made from Nitrogen and Hydrogen via the Haber process.

Question 14

What is the oxidation number of Oxygen in most of its compounds?

  • A. -1
  • B. -2
  • C. +2
  • D. 0
Answer: B. -2
Explanation: Oxygen usually accepts two electrons to reach a stable state.

Question 15

The indicator methyl orange turns ______ in acidic solutions.

  • A. Yellow
  • B. Blue
  • C. Red/Pink
  • D. Colorless
Answer: C. Red/Pink
Explanation: Methyl orange is red in acid and yellow in bases.

Question 16

Which of the following subatomic particles has no charge?

  • A. Proton
  • B. Electron
  • C. Neutron
  • D. Alpha particle
Answer: C. Neutron
Explanation: Neutrons are electrically neutral.

Question 17

What is the general formula for Alkanols?

  • A. CnH2n+2
  • B. CnH2n+1OH
  • C. CnH2n
  • D. CnH2n-2
Answer: B. CnH2n+1OH
Explanation: Alkanols are derived from alkanes by replacing one H with an -OH group.

Question 18

The temporary hardness of water can be removed by:

  • A. Filtration
  • B. Boiling
  • C. Chlorination
  • D. Decantation
Answer: B. Boiling
Explanation: Boiling decomposes Calcium Hydrogen Trioxocarbonate (IV) which causes temporary hardness.

Question 19

Which of the following is a transition metal?

  • A. Sodium
  • B. Calcium
  • C. Iron
  • D. Aluminum
Answer: C. Iron
Explanation: Iron is in the d-block of the periodic table, characteristic of transition metals.

Question 20

The noble gas used to provide an inert atmosphere in light bulbs is:

  • A. Helium
  • B. Neon
  • C. Argon
  • D. Radon
Answer: C. Argon
Explanation: Argon is unreactive and prevents the filament from burning out.

Question 21

An element with atomic number 9 is a:

  • A. Metal
  • B. Noble gas
  • C. Halogen
  • D. Alkali metal
Answer: C. Halogen
Explanation: Atomic number 9 is Fluorine, a member of the halogen group.

Question 22

Which type of bonding involves the "sea of electrons"?

  • A. Ionic
  • B. Covalent
  • C. Metallic
  • D. Dative
Answer: C. Metallic
Explanation: Metallic bonds consist of positive ions held together by a cloud of delocalized electrons.

Question 23

What color is the flame when Copper is heated?

  • A. Lilac
  • B. Golden yellow
  • C. Bluish-green
  • D. Brick red
Answer: C. Bluish-green
Explanation: Copper ions give a characteristic green or bluish-green color in a flame test.

Question 24

The process of converting vapor into liquid is called:

  • A. Evaporation
  • B. Sublimation
  • C. Condensation
  • D. Melting
Answer: C. Condensation
Explanation: Condensation is the reverse of evaporation.

Question 25

The catalyst used in the manufacture of sulfuric acid by the Contact process is:

  • A. Finely divided iron
  • B. Vanadium (V) oxide
  • C. Platinum
  • D. Manganese (IV) oxide
Answer: B. Vanadium (V) oxide
Explanation: V2O5 is the modern catalyst used for the oxidation of SO2 to SO3.

Question 26

What is the oxidation number of sulfur in H2S?

  • A. -2
  • B. +2
  • C. +4
  • D. +6
Answer: A. -2
Explanation: 2(+1) + S = 0. Therefore S = -2.

Question 27

Compounds with the same molecular formula but different structural arrangements are:

  • A. Isotopes
  • B. Allotropes
  • C. Isomers
  • D. Homologues
Answer: C. Isomers
Explanation: Isomerism is common in organic chemistry, such as butane and 2-methylpropane.

Question 28

Which of the following is a property of an endothermic reaction?

  • A. Heat is released
  • B. Temperature of surroundings increases
  • C. Enthalpy change is positive
  • D. It occurs spontaneously at all temperatures
Answer: C. Enthalpy change is positive
Explanation: Endothermic reactions absorb heat, so Delta H is positive.

Question 29

The gas that turns lime water milky is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Carbon (II) oxide
  • C. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • D. Nitrogen
Answer: C. Carbon (IV) oxide
Explanation: CO2 reacts with lime water to form insoluble Calcium Carbonate.

Question 30

Which of the following metals can be found free in nature?

  • A. Sodium
  • B. Calcium
  • C. Gold
  • D. Magnesium
Answer: C. Gold
Explanation: Gold is very unreactive (noble metal) and does not easily form compounds.

Question 31

The primary reason for galvanizing iron is to prevent:

  • A. Melting
  • B. Rusting
  • C. Evaporation
  • D. Conductivity
Answer: B. Rusting
Explanation: Zinc is coated on iron to protect it from moisture and oxygen.

Question 32

What is the pH of a solution containing 0.001 mol/dm3 of HCl?

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 3
  • D. 4
Answer: C. 3
Explanation: pH = -log[H+]. -log(10^-3) = 3.

Question 33

A mixture of sand and salt can be separated by:

  • A. Sublimation
  • B. Distillation
  • C. Dissolving in water, filtering, and evaporating
  • D. Chromatography
Answer: C. Dissolving in water, filtering, and evaporating
Explanation: Salt dissolves, sand does not. Filtering removes sand, and evaporation recovers salt.

Question 34

Which of the following is a natural polymer?

  • A. Nylon
  • B. Terylene
  • C. Cellulose
  • D. Polyethene
Answer: C. Cellulose
Explanation: Cellulose is a polymer of glucose found in plants.

Question 35

How many structural isomers does Butane (C4H10) have?

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 5
Answer: A. 2
Explanation: n-butane and 2-methylpropane.

Question 36

The gas used in the vulcanization of rubber is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Chlorine
  • C. Sulfur
  • D. Hydrogen
Answer: C. Sulfur
Explanation: Sulfur is added to rubber to make it tougher and more heat-resistant.

Question 37

Which subatomic particle orbits the nucleus?

  • A. Proton
  • B. Neutron
  • C. Electron
  • D. Nucleon
Answer: C. Electron
Explanation: Electrons are found in shells around the central nucleus.

Question 38

The functional group of Alkanoates (Esters) is:

  • A. -OH
  • B. -CHO
  • C. -COOR
  • D. -COOH
Answer: C. -COOR
Explanation: Alkanoates are formed by the reaction of an alcohol and an acid.

Question 39

The solubility of a gas in water generally ______ as temperature increases.

  • A. Increases
  • B. Decreases
  • C. Stays the same
  • D. Doubles
Answer: B. Decreases
Explanation: Gases escape the liquid faster as the temperature rises.

Question 40

Which of the following elements is a liquid at room temperature?

  • A. Chlorine
  • B. Bromine
  • C. Iodine
  • D. Fluorine
Answer: B. Bromine
Explanation: Bromine is one of the only two elements (the other is Mercury) that are liquid at standard conditions.

Question 41

In the reaction: Zn + CuSO4 -> ZnSO4 + Cu, Zinc is acting as a/an:

  • A. Oxidizing agent
  • B. Reducing agent
  • C. Catalyst
  • D. Dehydrating agent
Answer: B. Reducing agent
Explanation: Zinc loses electrons (is oxidized) and causes Copper ions to be reduced.

Question 42

Which of the following gases has a rotten egg smell?

  • A. NH3
  • B. SO2
  • C. H2S
  • D. CO2
Answer: C. H2S
Explanation: Hydrogen sulfide is notorious for its foul, rotten egg odor.

Question 43

The bond formed when an atom with a lone pair of electrons shares it with an electron-deficient atom is:

  • A. Covalent
  • B. Ionic
  • C. Dative (Coordinate)
  • D. Hydrogen
Answer: C. Dative (Coordinate)
Explanation: This occurs in the formation of the Ammonium ion (NH4+).

Question 44

What is the product formed at the cathode during the electrolysis of molten NaCl?

  • A. Chlorine gas
  • B. Sodium metal
  • C. Hydrogen gas
  • D. Oxygen gas
Answer: B. Sodium metal
Explanation: Sodium ions gain electrons (reduction) at the cathode to form neutral sodium atoms.

Question 45

Which of the following is an amphoteric oxide?

  • A. Na2O
  • B. CaO
  • C. ZnO
  • D. SO2
Answer: C. ZnO
Explanation: Zinc oxide reacts with both acids and strong bases.

Question 46

The major constituent of the gas used for domestic cooking (LPG) is:

  • A. Methane
  • B. Ethane
  • C. Butane
  • D. Pentane
Answer: C. Butane
Explanation: LPG is mostly a mixture of propane and butane.

Question 47

The term used to describe the heat content of a system is:

  • A. Entropy
  • B. Enthalpy
  • C. Energy
  • D. Power
Answer: B. Enthalpy
Explanation: Enthalpy (H) represents the total heat content of a chemical system.

Question 48

Which of the following is an example of a synthetic fiber?

  • A. Cotton
  • B. Wool
  • C. Nylon
  • D. Silk
Answer: C. Nylon
Explanation: Nylon is an artificially manufactured polyamide.

Question 49

Water gas is a mixture of:

  • A. CO and N2
  • B. CO and H2
  • C. CO2 and H2
  • D. CH4 and H2O
Answer: B. CO and H2
Explanation: Water gas is produced by passing steam over red-hot coke.

Question 50

Which of the following is the chief ore of lead?

  • A. Galena
  • B. Bauxite
  • C. Cassiterite
  • D. Haematite
Answer: A. Galena
Explanation: Galena is Lead (II) sulfide (PbS).
