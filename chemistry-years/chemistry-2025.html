Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

Which of the following separation techniques is most suitable for obtaining pure water from sea water?

  • A. Filtration
  • B. Evaporation
  • C. Distillation
  • D. Chromatography
Answer: C. Distillation
Explanation: Distillation involves boiling the water to create steam and then condensing it back into a liquid, leaving salts behind.

Question 2

The atomic number of an element is 13. What is its electronic configuration?

  • A. 2, 8, 1
  • B. 2, 8, 2
  • C. 2, 8, 3
  • D. 2, 8, 4
Answer: C. 2, 8, 3
Explanation: The first shell holds 2, the second holds 8, and the remaining 3 go to the third shell.

Question 3

Atoms of the same element with the same atomic number but different mass numbers are called:

  • A. Isomers
  • B. Allotropes
  • C. Isotopes
  • D. Isobars
Answer: C. Isotopes
Explanation: Isotopes have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.

Question 4

Which of the following represents a physical change?

  • A. Burning of wood
  • B. Rusting of iron
  • C. Melting of candle wax
  • D. Souring of milk
Answer: C. Melting of candle wax
Explanation: Melting is a change of state (solid to liquid) and can be reversed; no new substance is formed.

Question 5

Which of the following is a neutral oxide?

  • A. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • B. Sulfur (IV) oxide
  • C. Nitrogen (II) oxide
  • D. Sodium oxide
Answer: C. Nitrogen (II) oxide
Explanation: NO, N2O, and CO are common neutral oxides that do not react with acids or bases.

Question 6

The bond formed when two atoms share a pair of electrons is called:

  • A. Ionic bond
  • B. Covalent bond
  • C. Metallic bond
  • D. Hydrogen bond
Answer: B. Covalent bond
Explanation: Covalent bonding involves the sharing of valence electrons between non-metal atoms.

Question 7

Which of the following gas laws states that the volume of a fixed mass of gas is inversely proportional to its pressure at constant temperature?

  • A. Charles's Law
  • B. Boyle's Law
  • C. Graham's Law
  • D. Avogadro's Law
Answer: B. Boyle's Law
Explanation: Boyle's law is represented by the formula P1V1 = P2V2.

Question 8

What is the oxidation number of sulfur in H2SO4?

  • A. +2
  • B. +4
  • C. +6
  • D. -2
Answer: C. +6
Explanation: 2(1) + S + 4(-2) = 0. 2 + S - 8 = 0. S = +6.

Question 9

A solution with a pH of 3 is described as:

  • A. Weakly acidic
  • B. Strongly acidic
  • C. Weakly alkaline
  • D. Strongly alkaline
Answer: B. Strongly acidic
Explanation: pH values from 0 to 3 are generally considered strongly acidic.

Question 10

The process of a solid changing directly into a gas without passing through the liquid state is:

  • A. Evaporation
  • B. Sublimation
  • C. Condensation
  • D. Melting
Answer: B. Sublimation
Explanation: Iodine and Ammonium chloride are common substances that undergo sublimation.

Question 11

Which of the following is an example of an alloy?

  • A. Iron
  • B. Gold
  • C. Bronze
  • D. Copper
Answer: C. Bronze
Explanation: Bronze is an alloy consisting primarily of copper, usually with tin as the main additive.

Question 12

The main constituent of natural gas is:

  • A. Ethane
  • B. Methane
  • C. Propane
  • D. Butane
Answer: B. Methane
Explanation: Natural gas is composed of about 90 percent methane (CH4).

Question 13

Calculate the molar mass of CaCO3. (Ca=40, C=12, O=16)

  • A. 68 g/mol
  • B. 84 g/mol
  • C. 100 g/mol
  • D. 120 g/mol
Answer: C. 100 g/mol
Explanation: 40 + 12 + (3 * 16) = 40 + 12 + 48 = 100.

Question 14

Which of the following indicators gives a pink color in an alkaline solution?

  • A. Methyl orange
  • B. Litmus paper
  • C. Phenolphthalein
  • D. Universal indicator
Answer: C. Phenolphthalein
Explanation: Phenolphthalein is colorless in acid and pink in basic (alkaline) solutions.

Question 15

The most abundant gas in the atmosphere is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Argon
Answer: C. Nitrogen
Explanation: Nitrogen makes up approximately 78 percent of the Earth's atmosphere.

Question 16

Which of the following is an example of an endothermic reaction?

  • A. Combustion of wood
  • B. Neutralization of an acid
  • C. Thermal decomposition of limestone
  • D. Respiration
Answer: C. Thermal decomposition of limestone
Explanation: Endothermic reactions absorb heat from the surroundings to proceed.

Question 17

Hydrocarbons that contain only single bonds between carbon atoms are called:

  • A. Alkenes
  • B. Alkynes
  • C. Alkanes
  • D. Arenes
Answer: C. Alkanes
Explanation: Alkanes are saturated hydrocarbons with the general formula CnH2n+2.

Question 18

The catalyst used in the Haber process for the manufacture of ammonia is:

  • A. Vanadium (V) oxide
  • B. Finely divided iron
  • C. Platinum
  • D. Manganese (IV) oxide
Answer: B. Finely divided iron
Explanation: Iron is used to speed up the reaction between Nitrogen and Hydrogen in the Haber process.

Question 19

A chemical reaction that results in the gain of electrons is called:

  • A. Oxidation
  • B. Reduction
  • C. Electrolysis
  • D. Decomposition
Answer: B. Reduction
Explanation: Oxidation is Loss (OIL), Reduction is Gain (RIG) of electrons.

Question 20

Which of the following elements is a halogen?

  • A. Sodium
  • B. Magnesium
  • C. Chlorine
  • D. Neon
Answer: C. Chlorine
Explanation: Halogens belong to Group 17 (VII) and include Fluorine, Chlorine, Bromine, and Iodine.

Question 21

Water that does not form lather readily with soap is called:

  • A. Soft water
  • B. Distilled water
  • C. Hard water
  • D. Deionized water
Answer: C. Hard water
Explanation: Hardness is caused by the presence of dissolved Calcium and Magnesium ions.

Question 22

Which of the following is the functional group of alkanols?

  • A. -COOH
  • B. -CHO
  • C. -OH
  • D. -CO-
Answer: C. -OH
Explanation: The hydroxyl group (-OH) is the characteristic functional group of alcohols (alkanols).

Question 23

The number of moles in 5.85g of NaCl is: (Na=23, Cl=35.5)

  • A. 0.1 mol
  • B. 0.2 mol
  • C. 0.5 mol
  • D. 1.0 mol
Answer: A. 0.1 mol
Explanation: Molar mass of NaCl = 58.5. Moles = Mass / Molar mass = 5.85 / 58.5 = 0.1.

Question 24

Which type of bonding exists in Sodium Chloride?

  • A. Covalent
  • B. Electrovalent (Ionic)
  • C. Dative
  • D. Metallic
Answer: B. Electrovalent (Ionic)
Explanation: Sodium (metal) transfers an electron to Chlorine (non-metal).

Question 25

The maximum number of electrons that can be held in the M shell of an atom is:

  • A. 2
  • B. 8
  • C. 18
  • D. 32
Answer: C. 18
Explanation: Using the formula 2n², where n=3 for the M shell: 2(3)² = 18.

Question 26

Which gas is produced when dilute hydrochloric acid reacts with calcium trioxocarbonate (IV)?

  • A. Hydrogen
  • B. Chlorine
  • C. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • D. Oxygen
Answer: C. Carbon (IV) oxide
Explanation: Acids react with carbonates to produce salt, water, and CO2 gas.

Question 27

The process of coating iron with zinc to prevent rusting is called:

  • A. Tinning
  • B. Alloying
  • C. Galvanizing
  • D. Electroplating
Answer: C. Galvanizing
Explanation: Galvanizing provides sacrificial protection to the iron.

Question 28

Elements in the same group of the periodic table have the same:

  • A. Number of shells
  • B. Atomic radius
  • C. Number of valence electrons
  • D. Mass number
Answer: C. Number of valence electrons
Explanation: This gives them similar chemical properties.

Question 29

Which of the following is an example of a chemical change?

  • A. Evaporation of water
  • B. Sublimation of camphor
  • C. Fermentation of glucose
  • D. Filtration of a mixture
Answer: C. Fermentation of glucose
Explanation: Fermentation creates new substances (ethanol and CO2) and cannot be easily reversed.

Question 30

The molar volume of any gas at S.T.P. is:

  • A. 2.24 dm3
  • B. 22.4 dm3
  • C. 224 dm3
  • D. 24.0 dm3
Answer: B. 22.4 dm3
Explanation: Standard Temperature and Pressure volume for one mole of gas is 22.4 dm3.

Question 31

The flame color of Potassium when heated is:

  • A. Golden yellow
  • B. Lilac
  • C. Brick red
  • D. Apple green
Answer: B. Lilac
Explanation: Lilac is the characteristic flame color of Potassium ions.

Question 32

The radioisotope used in the treatment of cancer is:

  • A. Carbon-14
  • B. Iodine-131
  • C. Cobalt-60
  • D. Uranium-235
Answer: C. Cobalt-60
Explanation: Cobalt-60 emits gamma rays used to kill cancer cells.

Question 33

Anhydrous Copper (II) sulfate turns blue when added to:

  • A. Alcohol
  • B. Water
  • C. Benzene
  • D. Ether
Answer: B. Water
Explanation: This is a chemical test for the presence of water.

Question 34

Which of the following is a polymer?

  • A. Glucose
  • B. Propane
  • C. Starch
  • D. Ethanol
Answer: C. Starch
Explanation: Starch is a natural polymer made of glucose monomers.

Question 35

The rate of a chemical reaction can be increased by:

  • A. Decreasing temperature
  • B. Decreasing concentration
  • C. Increasing surface area of reactants
  • D. Increasing the size of the container
Answer: C. Increasing surface area of reactants
Explanation: More surface area allows for more frequent collisions between particles.

Question 36

Which gas is known as "laughing gas"?

  • A. Nitrogen (IV) oxide
  • B. Nitrogen (I) oxide
  • C. Nitrogen (II) oxide
  • D. Ammonia
Answer: B. Nitrogen (I) oxide
Explanation: N2O is known as laughing gas and is used in anesthesia.

Question 37

The product formed when ethanol reacts with ethanoic acid in the presence of an acid catalyst is:

  • A. Ethane
  • B. Ethyl ethanoate
  • C. Ethanal
  • D. Ethene
Answer: B. Ethyl ethanoate
Explanation: This is an esterification reaction.

Question 38

Which of the following is a saturated hydrocarbon?

  • A. C2H2
  • B. C2H4
  • C. C3H8
  • D. C6H6
Answer: C. C3H8
Explanation: Propane (C3H8) fits the alkane formula CnH2n+2.

Question 39

The noble gas used in filling advertising signs is:

  • A. Helium
  • B. Neon
  • C. Argon
  • D. Krypton
Answer: B. Neon
Explanation: Neon gives a bright reddish-orange glow when an electric discharge is passed through it.

Question 40

The substance that turns lime water milky is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Carbon (II) oxide
  • C. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • D. Hydrogen
Answer: C. Carbon (IV) oxide
Explanation: CO2 reacts with Calcium hydroxide to form insoluble Calcium carbonate.

Question 41

Which of the following is a primary cell?

  • A. Lead-acid accumulator
  • B. Leclanche cell
  • C. Fuel cell
  • D. Nickel-cadmium cell
Answer: B. Leclanche cell
Explanation: Primary cells cannot be recharged once they go flat.

Question 42

The monomer of natural rubber is:

  • A. Ethene
  • B. Isoprene
  • C. Butadiene
  • D. Styrene
Answer: B. Isoprene
Explanation: Natural rubber is polyisoprene.

Question 43

Which of the following elements has the highest electronegativity?

  • A. Chlorine
  • B. Fluorine
  • C. Oxygen
  • D. Nitrogen
Answer: B. Fluorine
Explanation: Fluorine is the most electronegative element on the periodic table.

Question 44

The shape of a water molecule is:

  • A. Linear
  • B. Tetrahedral
  • C. Bent (V-shaped)
  • D. Trigonal planar
Answer: C. Bent (V-shaped)
Explanation: Due to the presence of two lone pairs on the Oxygen atom.

Question 45

Which of the following is not a greenhouse gas?

  • A. Methane
  • B. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Water vapor
Answer: C. Nitrogen
Explanation: Nitrogen does not trap heat in the atmosphere like greenhouse gases do.

Question 46

The gas used in the vulcanization of rubber is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Sulfur
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Chlorine
Answer: B. Sulfur
Explanation: Sulfur forms cross-links between rubber chains to make it tougher.

Question 47

Which of the following is a heavy metal?

  • A. Sodium
  • B. Lead
  • C. Aluminium
  • D. Magnesium
Answer: B. Lead
Explanation: Lead has a high density and high atomic mass.

Question 48

The enthalpy change for a reaction where heat is released is:

  • A. Positive
  • B. Negative
  • C. Zero
  • D. Undefined
Answer: B. Negative
Explanation: Exothermic reactions have a negative Delta H.

Question 49

Which of the following is the main ore of aluminium?

  • A. Cassiterite
  • B. Bauxite
  • C. Haematite
  • D. Galena
Answer: B. Bauxite
Explanation: Bauxite consists mostly of aluminium oxide compounds.

Question 50

The functional group present in ethanoic acid is:

  • A. Hydroxyl
  • B. Carbonyl
  • C. Carboxyl
  • D. Ester
Answer: C. Carboxyl
Explanation: The -COOH group is the carboxyl group.
