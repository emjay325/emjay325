Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

Which of the following elements has the electronic configuration 1s2 2s2 2p6 3s2 3p3?

  • A. Nitrogen
  • B. Phosphorus
  • C. Sulfur
  • D. Chlorine
Answer: B. Phosphorus
Explanation: The total number of electrons is 2+2+6+2+3 = 15. The element with atomic number 15 is Phosphorus.

Question 2

The isotope of Hydrogen that contains no neutrons in its nucleus is:

  • A. Deuterium
  • B. Tritium
  • C. Protium
  • D. Helium
Answer: C. Protium
Explanation: Protium (1H1) has 1 proton and 0 neutrons. Deuterium has 1, and Tritium has 2.

Question 3

Which of the following forces is responsible for the low boiling points of noble gases?

  • A. Covalent bonds
  • B. Ionic bonds
  • C. Van der Waals forces
  • D. Hydrogen bonds
Answer: C. Van der Waals forces
Explanation: Noble gases are monoatomic and are held together by very weak induced dipole-dipole attractions (Van der Waals forces).

Question 4

The geometric shape of the Carbon (IV) oxide molecule is:

  • A. V-shaped
  • B. Linear
  • C. Tetrahedral
  • D. Trigonal planar
Answer: B. Linear
Explanation: CO2 has two double bonds and no lone pairs on the carbon atom, resulting in a 180-degree bond angle.

Question 5

As we move from left to right across a period in the periodic table, the atomic radius:

  • A. Increases
  • B. Decreases
  • C. Remains constant
  • D. Increases then decreases
Answer: B. Decreases
Explanation: Increased nuclear charge pulls the electron shells closer to the nucleus, reducing the radius.

Question 6

Which of the following is most suitable for separating the components of ink?

  • A. Filtration
  • B. Distillation
  • C. Paper Chromatography
  • D. Crystallization
Answer: C. Paper Chromatography
Explanation: Chromatography separates mixtures based on the different rates at which components travel through a stationary phase.

Question 7

A sample of a substance is considered pure if it has a:

  • A. Variable boiling point
  • B. Fixed melting point
  • C. High density
  • D. Different color after heating
Answer: B. Fixed melting point
Explanation: Pure substances have sharp, constant melting and boiling points, unlike mixtures.

Question 8

The equation PV = nRT represents the:

  • A. Boyle's Law
  • B. Charles's Law
  • C. Ideal Gas Law
  • D. Dalton's Law
Answer: C. Ideal Gas Law
Explanation: This equation combines Boyle's, Charles's, and Avogadro's laws into a single relationship.

Question 9

Compare the rates of diffusion of Hydrogen (H2) and Oxygen (O2). (H=1, O=16)

  • A. Hydrogen diffuses 4 times faster
  • B. Oxygen diffuses 4 times faster
  • C. Hydrogen diffuses 16 times faster
  • D. They diffuse at the same rate
Answer: A. Hydrogen diffuses 4 times faster
Explanation: Rate is inversely proportional to the square root of the molar mass. sqrt(32/2) = sqrt(16) = 4.

Question 10

Ammonium chloride can be separated from Sodium chloride by:

  • A. Decantation
  • B. Evaporation
  • C. Sublimation
  • D. Magnetism
Answer: C. Sublimation
Explanation: Ammonium chloride changes directly from solid to gas when heated, leaving the salt behind.

Question 11

Which of the following is an example of a chemical change?

  • A. Dissolving sugar in water
  • B. Magnetizing an iron nail
  • C. Burning magnesium ribbon
  • D. Melting ice
Answer: C. Burning magnesium ribbon
Explanation: Burning magnesium creates a new substance, Magnesium oxide, through a non-reversible reaction.

Question 12

The maximum number of electrons that can occupy the 3p subshell is:

  • A. 2
  • B. 6
  • C. 10
  • D. 14
Answer: B. 6
Explanation: The p subshell always contains 3 orbitals, each holding 2 electrons, for a total of 6.

Question 13

Which of the following is a physical property of Chlorine gas?

  • A. It bleaches moist litmus paper
  • B. It reacts with metals
  • C. It is a greenish-yellow gas
  • D. It supports combustion
Answer: C. It is a greenish-yellow gas
Explanation: Color is a physical property. The other options describe chemical reactions (bleaching and reacting).

Question 14

What is the oxidation number of Nitrogen in the NH4+ ion?

  • A. -3
  • B. +3
  • C. -5
  • D. +1
Answer: A. -3
Explanation: N + 4(+1) = +1. N + 4 = 1. N = -3.

Question 15

The standard molar volume of a gas is measured at 0 degrees Celsius and ______ pressure.

  • A. 1.0 atm
  • B. 2.0 atm
  • C. 10.0 atm
  • D. 0.5 atm
Answer: A. 1.0 atm
Explanation: Standard pressure is 1 atmosphere (or 760 mmHg).

Question 16

The solubility of KNO3 in water ______ as the temperature increases.

  • A. Decreases
  • B. Remains constant
  • C. Increases
  • D. Becomes zero
Answer: C. Increases
Explanation: For most solid salts like Potassium nitrate, solubility is directly proportional to temperature.

Question 17

Which of the following is a neutral oxide?

  • A. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • B. Nitrogen (I) oxide
  • C. Sulfur (IV) oxide
  • D. Calcium oxide
Answer: B. Nitrogen (I) oxide
Explanation: N2O (laughing gas), NO, and CO are neutral oxides that do not react with acids or bases.

Question 18

A basic solution has a pH value:

  • A. Equal to 7
  • B. Less than 7
  • C. Greater than 7
  • D. Equal to 0
Answer: C. Greater than 7
Explanation: Acids are below 7, neutral is 7, and bases (alkalis) are above 7.

Question 19

The term "water of crystallization" refers to water that is:

  • A. Used to dissolve a salt
  • B. Found in the atmosphere
  • C. Chemically combined in the crystal structure of a salt
  • D. Produced during a neutralization reaction
Answer: C. Chemically combined in the crystal structure of a salt
Explanation: Example: CuSO4.5H2O, where the 5H2O is the water of crystallization.

Question 20

The process of adding Chlorine to water during treatment is to:

  • A. Remove dirt
  • B. Make the water clear
  • C. Kill harmful bacteria
  • D. Soften the water
Answer: C. Kill harmful bacteria
Explanation: Chlorination is the sterilization step in water purification.

Question 21

Which of the following is the most reactive halogen?

  • A. Fluorine
  • B. Chlorine
  • C. Bromine
  • D. Iodine
Answer: A. Fluorine
Explanation: Reactivity of halogens decreases down the group. Fluorine is the most electronegative and reactive.

Question 22

The energy required to remove the most loosely bound electron from a gaseous atom is:

  • A. Electron affinity
  • B. Electronegativity
  • C. Ionization energy
  • D. Activation energy
Answer: C. Ionization energy
Explanation: This is the formal definition of first ionization energy.

Question 23

Which of the following elements is a transition metal?

  • A. Calcium
  • B. Aluminum
  • C. Copper
  • D. Sodium
Answer: C. Copper
Explanation: Copper belongs to the d-block elements, which are the transition metals.

Question 24

During the electrolysis of Copper (II) tetraoxosulfate (VI) using copper electrodes, the mass of the anode:

  • A. Increases
  • B. Decreases
  • C. Remains constant
  • D. Doubles
Answer: B. Decreases
Explanation: The copper anode dissolves into the solution as Cu2+ ions.

Question 25

A catalyst increases the rate of reaction by:

  • A. Increasing the temperature
  • B. Increasing the collision frequency
  • C. Lowering the activation energy
  • D. Increasing the pressure
Answer: C. Lowering the activation energy
Explanation: A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway with a lower energy barrier.

Question 26

The functional group of the Alkanoic acid series is:

  • A. -OH
  • B. -CHO
  • C. -COOH
  • D. -CO-
Answer: C. -COOH
Explanation: This is the carboxyl group characteristic of organic acids like ethanoic acid.

Question 27

Which of the following is an unsaturated hydrocarbon?

  • A. CH4
  • B. C2H6
  • C. C2H4
  • D. C3H8
Answer: C. C2H4
Explanation: Ethene (C2H4) contains a double bond, making it unsaturated. The others are alkanes.

Question 28

The process by which large molecules are broken down into smaller ones by heat is:

  • A. Polymerization
  • B. Cracking
  • C. Saponification
  • D. Esterification
Answer: B. Cracking
Explanation: Cracking is used in petroleum refining to produce petrol from heavier fractions.

Question 29

Which gas is used in the manufacture of margarine from vegetable oils?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Nitrogen
  • C. Hydrogen
  • D. Chlorine
Answer: C. Hydrogen
Explanation: The process is called hydrogenation, which hardens the oil into a solid fat.

Question 30

What is the IUPAC name of the compound CH3CH(CH3)CH2CH3?

  • A. Pentane
  • B. 2-methylbutane
  • C. 3-methylbutane
  • D. Isopentane
Answer: B. 2-methylbutane
Explanation: The longest chain has 4 carbons (butane) with a methyl group on the second carbon.

Question 31

Which of the following is a synthetic polymer?

  • A. Starch
  • B. Cellulose
  • C. Nylon
  • D. Proteins
Answer: C. Nylon
Explanation: Nylon is man-made; starch, cellulose, and proteins are natural polymers.

Question 32

The reaction between an organic acid and an alcohol to produce an ester is called:

  • A. Saponification
  • B. Esterification
  • C. Hydrolysis
  • D. Hydration
Answer: B. Esterification
Explanation: Acid + Alcohol -> Ester + Water.

Question 33

Which of the following is a use of Carbon (IV) oxide?

  • A. Filling weather balloons
  • B. As a fuel in engines
  • C. In fire extinguishers
  • D. For respiration in animals
Answer: C. In fire extinguishers
Explanation: CO2 is denser than air and does not support combustion, making it ideal for smothering fires.

Question 34

The temporary hardness of water is caused by the presence of:

  • A. Calcium tetraoxosulfate (VI)
  • B. Magnesium trioxocarbonate (IV)
  • C. Calcium hydrogen trioxocarbonate (IV)
  • D. Sodium chloride
Answer: C. Calcium hydrogen trioxocarbonate (IV)
Explanation: This salt can be removed by boiling, which is why it is called "temporary" hardness.

Question 35

The method used to extract Aluminum from Bauxite is:

  • A. Reduction with carbon
  • B. Thermal decomposition
  • C. Electrolysis of molten Alumina
  • D. Displacement by Iron
Answer: C. Electrolysis of molten Alumina
Explanation: Aluminum is too reactive to be reduced by carbon, so the Hall-Heroult electrolytic process is used.

Question 36

Which of the following gases is produced when coal burns in a limited supply of air?

  • A. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • B. Carbon (II) oxide
  • C. Sulfur (IV) oxide
  • D. Methane
Answer: B. Carbon (II) oxide
Explanation: Incomplete combustion produces CO (Carbon monoxide), which is highly poisonous.

Question 37

The percentage by mass of Carbon in Ethanol (C2H5OH) is: (C=12, H=1, O=16)

  • A. 52.2 percent
  • B. 26.1 percent
  • C. 13.0 percent
  • D. 46.0 percent
Answer: A. 52.2 percent
Explanation: Molar mass = 46. Carbon mass = 24. (24/46) * 100 = 52.17.

Question 38

Which of the following is a property of graphite?

  • A. It is extremely hard
  • B. It is used as an abrasive
  • C. It conducts electricity
  • D. It has a tetrahedral structure
Answer: C. It conducts electricity
Explanation: Graphite has delocalized electrons between its hexagonal layers.

Question 39

The catalyst used in the manufacture of Sulfuric acid by the Contact process is:

  • A. Finely divided Iron
  • B. Vanadium (V) oxide
  • C. Manganese (IV) oxide
  • D. Nickel
Answer: B. Vanadium (V) oxide
Explanation: V2O5 is used to catalyze the oxidation of SO2 to SO3.

Question 40

Hydrocarbons that undergo only substitution reactions are:

  • A. Alkanes
  • B. Alkenes
  • C. Alkynes
  • D. Alkanols
Answer: A. Alkanes
Explanation: Alkanes are saturated and lack double/triple bonds, so they cannot perform addition reactions.

Question 41

Which of the following ions is discharged first at the cathode during the electrolysis of brine?

  • A. Sodium ion
  • B. Hydrogen ion
  • C. Hydroxyl ion
  • D. Chloride ion
Answer: B. Hydrogen ion
Explanation: Hydrogen is lower in the electrochemical series than Sodium, so it is preferred for discharge.

Question 42

The major air pollutant responsible for acid rain is:

  • A. Carbon (II) oxide
  • B. Methane
  • C. Sulfur (IV) oxide
  • D. Helium
Answer: C. Sulfur (IV) oxide
Explanation: SO2 reacts with atmospheric moisture to form sulfurous and sulfuric acid.

Question 43

Which of the following is the chief ore of Tin?

  • A. Bauxite
  • B. Cassiterite
  • C. Galena
  • D. Haematite
Answer: B. Cassiterite
Explanation: Cassiterite is SnO2.

Question 44

What color change is observed when SO2 is bubbled through acidified K2Cr2O7?

  • A. Purple to colorless
  • B. Orange to green
  • C. Blue to red
  • D. Yellow to brown
Answer: B. Orange to green
Explanation: Potassium dichromate is reduced from Cr(VI) (orange) to Cr(III) (green).

Question 45

The noble gas used in filling deep-sea diving tanks along with Oxygen is:

  • A. Argon
  • B. Neon
  • C. Helium
  • D. Xenon
Answer: C. Helium
Explanation: Helium is used because it is less soluble in blood than nitrogen, preventing "the bends."

Question 46

A coordinate covalent bond is found in:

  • A. NaCl
  • B. MgCl2
  • C. NH4+
  • D. CH4
Answer: C. NH4+
Explanation: The Ammonium ion is formed when Ammonia (NH3) shares its lone pair with a Hydrogen ion (H+).

Question 47

Which of the following is an allotrope of Oxygen?

  • A. Ozone
  • B. Water
  • C. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • D. Sulfur
Answer: A. Ozone
Explanation: Ozone (O3) and Oxygen gas (O2) are the two primary allotropes of oxygen.

Question 48

The most abundant element in the universe is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Nitrogen
  • C. Hydrogen
  • D. Helium
Answer: C. Hydrogen
Explanation: Hydrogen makes up about 75 percent of the elemental mass of the universe.

Question 49

Ethanoic acid reacts with Sodium trioxocarbonate (IV) to release:

  • A. Hydrogen gas
  • B. Oxygen gas
  • C. Carbon (IV) oxide gas
  • D. Nitrogen gas
Answer: C. Carbon (IV) oxide gas
Explanation: Reaction of any acid with a carbonate releases salt, water, and CO2.

Question 50

The ratio of the mass of a volume of gas to the mass of an equal volume of Hydrogen is its:

  • A. Relative atomic mass
  • B. Molar mass
  • C. Vapor density
  • D. Solubility
Answer: C. Vapor density
Explanation: Vapor density is defined as the mass of a certain volume of gas divided by the mass of the same volume of Hydrogen.
