Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

Which of the following elements has the electronic configuration 2, 8, 8, 2?

  • A. Magnesium
  • B. Calcium
  • C. Potassium
  • D. Aluminum
Answer: B. Calcium
Explanation: Total number of electrons = 20. Atomic number 20 is Calcium.

Question 2

The periodic table is arranged in order of increasing:

  • A. Atomic mass
  • B. Atomic number
  • C. Neutron number
  • D. Mass number
Answer: B. Atomic number
Explanation: Modern periodic law states that properties of elements are periodic functions of their atomic numbers.

Question 3

Which of the following is a redox reaction?

  • A. NaOH + HCl -> NaCl + H2O
  • B. AgNO3 + NaCl -> AgCl + NaNO3
  • C. H2 + Cl2 -> 2HCl
  • D. CaCO3 -> CaO + CO2
Answer: C. H2 + Cl2 -> 2HCl
Explanation: Hydrogen and Chlorine both change their oxidation states from 0 to +1 and -1 respectively.

Question 4

The type of bond in which electrons are shared between atoms of the same electronegativity is:

  • A. Polar covalent
  • B. Non-polar covalent
  • C. Ionic
  • D. Coordinate
Answer: B. Non-polar covalent
Explanation: Equal sharing of electrons occurs when the atoms have the same or very similar electronegativity values.

Question 5

What volume will 0.5 moles of a gas occupy at S.T.P.?

  • A. 11.2 dm3
  • B. 22.4 dm3
  • C. 44.8 dm3
  • D. 5.6 dm3
Answer: A. 11.2 dm3
Explanation: 1 mole occupies 22.4 dm3 at S.T.P. Therefore, 0.5 moles occupies 22.4 * 0.5 = 11.2 dm3.

Question 6

Which of the following separation methods is based on the difference in boiling points?

  • A. Filtration
  • B. Decantation
  • C. Fractional distillation
  • D. Crystallization
Answer: C. Fractional distillation
Explanation: This method separates miscible liquids based on their different boiling points.

Question 7

The atomic number of an element is the number of ______ in its nucleus.

  • A. Neutrons
  • B. Protons
  • C. Electrons
  • D. Nucleons
Answer: B. Protons
Explanation: Atomic number is defined strictly by the number of protons.

Question 8

What is the oxidation number of Sulfur in SO2?

  • A. +2
  • B. +4
  • C. +6
  • D. -2
Answer: B. +4
Explanation: S + 2(-2) = 0. S - 4 = 0. S = +4.

Question 9

Which of the following is a greenhouse gas?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Nitrogen
  • C. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • D. Helium
Answer: C. Carbon (IV) oxide
Explanation: CO2 traps heat in the atmosphere, leading to the greenhouse effect.

Question 10

The gas produced when dilute HCl reacts with Magnesium metal is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Chlorine
  • C. Hydrogen
  • D. Nitrogen
Answer: C. Hydrogen
Explanation: Active metals react with dilute acids to displace Hydrogen gas.

Question 11

A property of a pure substance that remains constant regardless of the sample size is:

  • A. Mass
  • B. Volume
  • C. Density
  • D. Weight
Answer: C. Density
Explanation: Density is an intensive property; it does not change based on how much material you have.

Question 12

The process of soap making is known as:

  • A. Esterification
  • B. Saponification
  • C. Fermentation
  • D. Polymerization
Answer: B. Saponification
Explanation: Saponification is the alkaline hydrolysis of fats or oils to produce soap.

Question 13

Which of the following allotropes of Carbon is the hardest known natural substance?

  • A. Graphite
  • B. Diamond
  • C. Coal
  • D. Lampblack
Answer: B. Diamond
Explanation: The tetrahedral arrangement of carbon atoms in diamond makes it extremely hard.

Question 14

The number of atoms in one mole of any substance is known as:

  • A. Atomic number
  • B. Avogadro's number
  • C. Mass number
  • D. Relative atomic mass
Answer: B. Avogadro's number
Explanation: Avogadro's constant is approximately 6.02 x 10^23 particles per mole.

Question 15

Which of the following indicators is colorless in acidic solution?

  • A. Methyl orange
  • B. Phenolphthalein
  • C. Litmus paper
  • D. Universal indicator
Answer: B. Phenolphthalein
Explanation: Phenolphthalein is colorless in acids and turns pink in bases.

Question 16

What is the functional group of Alkanols?

  • A. -COOH
  • B. -CHO
  • C. -OH
  • D. -CO-
Answer: C. -OH
Explanation: The hydroxyl group (-OH) is the characteristic group for alcohols.

Question 17

Which gas law relates volume and temperature at constant pressure?

  • A. Boyle's Law
  • B. Charles's Law
  • C. Avogadro's Law
  • D. Graham's Law
Answer: B. Charles's Law
Explanation: V1/T1 = V2/T2.

Question 18

Hard water contains dissolved ions of:

  • A. Sodium and Potassium
  • B. Calcium and Magnesium
  • C. Lead and Copper
  • D. Iron and Zinc
Answer: B. Calcium and Magnesium
Explanation: The presence of Ca2+ and Mg2+ ions prevents soap from lathering easily.

Question 19

The enzyme that breaks down starch into maltose is:

  • A. Pepsin
  • B. Lipase
  • C. Amylase
  • D. Trypsin
Answer: C. Amylase
Explanation: Amylase is found in saliva and pancreatic juice.

Question 20

The noble gas used in filling advertising neon signs is:

  • A. Argon
  • B. Helium
  • C. Neon
  • D. Radon
Answer: C. Neon
Explanation: Neon emits a bright red-orange glow when an electric current passes through it.

Question 21

Which of the following is a base?

  • A. NaCl
  • B. H2SO4
  • C. KOH
  • D. CH4
Answer: C. KOH
Explanation: Potassium hydroxide is an alkali (a soluble base).

Question 22

The heat change when one mole of a substance is formed from its elements is called enthalpy of:

  • A. Combustion
  • B. Neutralization
  • C. Formation
  • D. Solution
Answer: C. Formation
Explanation: Enthalpy of formation is the heat change when a compound is formed from its constituent elements in their standard states.

Question 23

What is the molar mass of NaOH? (Na=23, O=16, H=1)

  • A. 30 g/mol
  • B. 40 g/mol
  • C. 50 g/mol
  • D. 23 g/mol
Answer: B. 40 g/mol
Explanation: 23 + 16 + 1 = 40.

Question 24

Which of the following gases turns moist blue litmus paper red and then bleaches it?

  • A. Sulfur (IV) oxide
  • B. Chlorine
  • C. Ammonia
  • D. Hydrogen chloride
Answer: B. Chlorine
Explanation: Chlorine is acidic (turns blue litmus red) and is also a powerful bleaching agent.

Question 25

Elements in Group 1 of the periodic table are called:

  • A. Halogens
  • B. Noble gases
  • C. Alkali metals
  • D. Alkaline earth metals
Answer: C. Alkali metals
Explanation: Group 1 contains metals like Sodium and Potassium which are highly reactive.

Question 26

Which of the following is an electrolyte?

  • A. Sugar solution
  • B. Ethanol
  • C. Copper (II) tetraoxosulfate (VI) solution
  • D. Kerosene
Answer: C. Copper (II) tetraoxosulfate (VI) solution
Explanation: Electrolytes are substances that dissociate into ions in solution and conduct electricity.

Question 27

What type of reaction is: C2H4 + H2 -> C2H6?

  • A. Substitution
  • B. Addition
  • C. Elimination
  • D. Decomposition
Answer: B. Addition
Explanation: This is an addition reaction where an unsaturated compound becomes saturated.

Question 28

The Brownian motion provides evidence for the ______ nature of matter.

  • A. Wave
  • B. Continuous
  • C. Particulate
  • D. Stationary
Answer: C. Particulate
Explanation: The random motion of particles suggests that matter is made of tiny, moving particles.

Question 29

Which of the following elements is a halogen?

  • A. Sodium
  • B. Aluminum
  • C. Iodine
  • D. Neon
Answer: C. Iodine
Explanation: Halogens belong to Group 17 (VII) and include Fluorine, Chlorine, Bromine, and Iodine.

Question 30

The oxidation state of an element in its free uncombined state is:

  • A. +1
  • B. -1
  • C. 0
  • D. +2
Answer: C. 0
Explanation: By rule, the oxidation number of any pure element is zero.

Question 31

Temporary hardness in water is caused by the presence of:

  • A. Calcium trioxocarbonate (IV)
  • B. Calcium hydrogen trioxocarbonate (IV)
  • C. Magnesium tetraoxosulfate (VI)
  • D. Sodium chloride
Answer: B. Calcium hydrogen trioxocarbonate (IV)
Explanation: This compound decomposes upon boiling, which removes the hardness.

Question 32

The IUPAC name for CH3COOH is:

  • A. Methanoic acid
  • B. Ethanoic acid
  • C. Propanoic acid
  • D. Butanoic acid
Answer: B. Ethanoic acid
Explanation: A two-carbon chain acid is named ethanoic acid.

Question 33

Anhydrous copper (II) tetraoxosulfate (VI) is used as a test for water because it turns from white to ______ in the presence of water.

  • A. Green
  • B. Red
  • C. Blue
  • D. Black
Answer: C. Blue
Explanation: Hydrated copper (II) sulfate is blue.

Question 34

What is the percentage of Oxygen in CO2? (C=12, O=16)

  • A. 27.3 percent
  • B. 72.7 percent
  • C. 50 percent
  • D. 12 percent
Answer: B. 72.7 percent
Explanation: Molar mass = 12 + 32 = 44. %O = (32 / 44) * 100 = 72.7 percent.

Question 35

Which of the following is a physical change?

  • A. Burning of paper
  • B. Digestion of food
  • C. Sublimation of Iodine
  • D. Fermentation
Answer: C. Sublimation of Iodine
Explanation: Sublimation is a change of state, which is a physical process.

Question 36

The monomer of polyethene is:

  • A. Methane
  • B. Ethene
  • C. Ethyne
  • D. Propene
Answer: B. Ethene
Explanation: Ethene molecules join together to form the polymer polyethene.

Question 37

The mass number of an element is the sum of ______ and ______.

  • A. Protons and Electrons
  • B. Protons and Neutrons
  • C. Neutrons and Electrons
  • D. Protons and Nucleons
Answer: B. Protons and Neutrons
Explanation: These are the heavy particles located in the nucleus.

Question 38

Which of the following is a neutral oxide?

  • A. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • B. Carbon (II) oxide
  • C. Sulfur (IV) oxide
  • D. Sodium oxide
Answer: B. Carbon (II) oxide
Explanation: CO, N2O, and NO are neutral oxides.

Question 39

A catalyst increases the rate of reaction by lowering the:

  • A. Enthalpy of reaction
  • B. Temperature
  • C. Activation energy
  • D. Concentration
Answer: C. Activation energy
Explanation: It provides an alternative pathway with a lower energy barrier for the reaction to occur.

Question 40

Which metal is extracted using the blast furnace?

  • A. Aluminum
  • B. Copper
  • C. Iron
  • D. Sodium
Answer: C. Iron
Explanation: Iron is extracted from its ores (like Haematite) in the blast furnace.

Question 41

The bond formed when one atom provides both shared electrons is a ______ bond.

  • A. Ionic
  • B. Covalent
  • C. Dative (Coordinate)
  • D. Metallic
Answer: C. Dative (Coordinate)
Explanation: This occurs in ions like NH4+ or H3O+.

Question 42

Which of the following is an alkaline gas?

  • A. CO2
  • B. NH3
  • C. SO2
  • D. Cl2
Answer: B. NH3
Explanation: Ammonia is the only common alkaline gas.

Question 43

The process of converting a liquid to a vapor by heating and then condensing the vapor back to a liquid is:

  • A. Filtration
  • B. Sublimation
  • C. Distillation
  • D. Evaporation
Answer: C. Distillation
Explanation: Distillation combines evaporation and condensation.

Question 44

A gas that is lighter than air and used in filling balloons is:

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Helium
  • C. Carbon (IV) oxide
  • D. Chlorine
Answer: B. Helium
Explanation: Helium is less dense than air and safe (non-flammable).

Question 45

What is the pH of a neutral solution at 25 degrees Celsius?

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. 7
  • D. 14
Answer: C. 7
Explanation: On the pH scale of 0-14, 7 is the neutral point.

Question 46

The most abundant element in the Earth's crust is:

  • A. Silicon
  • B. Iron
  • C. Oxygen
  • D. Aluminum
Answer: C. Oxygen
Explanation: Oxygen makes up nearly half of the weight of the Earth's crust.

Question 47

Which of the following is used in the manufacture of margarine?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Hydrogen
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Chlorine
Answer: B. Hydrogen
Explanation: Hydrogenation of vegetable oils produces margarine.

Question 48

The standard unit of Pressure is:

  • A. Kelvin
  • B. Joule
  • C. Pascal
  • D. Mole
Answer: C. Pascal
Explanation: Pascal (Pa) is the S.I. unit for pressure.

Question 49

What is the general formula for Alkenes?

  • A. CnH2n+2
  • B. CnH2n
  • C. CnH2n-2
  • D. CnHn
Answer: B. CnH2n
Explanation: Alkenes are unsaturated hydrocarbons with one double bond.

Question 50

The molar volume of any gas at S.T.P. is approximately ______ dm3.

  • A. 2.24
  • B. 22.4
  • C. 24.0
  • D. 1.0
Answer: B. 22.4
Explanation: At standard temperature and pressure, one mole of an ideal gas occupies 22.4 liters (dm3).
