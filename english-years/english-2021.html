Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

SECTION 1: SYNONYMS

Choose the word or group of words nearest in meaning to the underlined word.

Question 1

The details of the plan were obscure even to the staff.

  • A. hidden
  • B. clear
  • C. famous
  • D. difficult
Answer: A. hidden
Explanation: Obscure means not discovered or known about; uncertain or hidden.

Question 2

The widow began to lament the loss of her husband.

  • A. celebrate
  • B. mourn
  • C. ignore
  • D. forget
Answer: B. mourn
Explanation: To lament means to express passionate grief or sorrow; to mourn.

Question 3

Success comes to those who are diligent in their studies.

  • A. lazy
  • B. wealthy
  • C. hard-working
  • D. clever
Answer: C. hard-working
Explanation: Diligent means having or showing care and conscientiousness in one's work; hard-working.

Question 4

We need a pragmatic approach to solve this traffic problem.

  • A. practical
  • B. theoretical
  • C. fast
  • D. expensive
Answer: A. practical
Explanation: Pragmatic means dealing with things sensibly and realistically; practical.

Question 5

The editor decided to abridge the long manuscript.

  • A. lengthen
  • B. shorten
  • C. destroy
  • D. publish
Answer: B. shorten
Explanation: To abridge means to shorten a piece of writing without losing the sense.

SECTION 2: ANTONYMS

Choose the word or group of words opposite in meaning to the underlined word.

Question 11

The meeting ended in total chaos.

  • A. noise
  • B. order
  • C. confusion
  • D. fighting
Answer: B. order
Explanation: Chaos means complete disorder and confusion; the opposite is order.

Question 12

The new manager is very arrogant.

  • A. proud
  • B. humble
  • C. wealthy
  • D. rude
Answer: B. humble
Explanation: Arrogant means having an exaggerated sense of one's own importance; the opposite is humble.

Question 13

He was known for being stingy with his resources.

  • A. mean
  • B. generous
  • C. careful
  • D. quiet
Answer: B. generous
Explanation: Stingy means unwilling to give or spend; the opposite is generous.

Question 14

The wall was painted with vertical stripes.

  • A. upright
  • B. horizontal
  • C. diagonal
  • D. crooked
Answer: B. horizontal
Explanation: Vertical means up-and-down; the opposite is horizontal (left-to-right).

Question 15

They found ancient artifacts in the desert.

  • A. old
  • B. modern
  • C. valuable
  • D. dusty
Answer: B. modern
Explanation: Ancient means belonging to the very distant past; the opposite is modern.

SECTION 3: IDIOMS AND INTERPRETATION

Choose the most appropriate interpretation for the idiomatic expression.

Question 21

Don't be a wet blanket at the party tonight.

  • A. a person who is wet
  • B. someone who spoils the fun
  • C. someone who brings a blanket
  • D. a very helpful person
Answer: B. someone who spoils the fun
Explanation: A "wet blanket" is a person who discourages enjoyment or enthusiasm.

Question 22

The manager turned a deaf ear to our complaints.

  • A. became deaf
  • B. refused to listen
  • C. listened carefully
  • D. shouted at us
Answer: B. refused to listen
Explanation: To "turn a deaf ear" means to refuse to listen to something or ignore it.

Question 23

Investments in education will pay off in the long run.

  • A. after a marathon
  • B. eventually
  • C. immediately
  • D. never
Answer: B. eventually
Explanation: "In the long run" means over a long period of time or in the end.

Question 24

When he moved to the city, he felt like a fish out of water.

  • A. very thirsty
  • B. uncomfortable in a new situation
  • C. happy to be free
  • D. ready to swim
Answer: B. uncomfortable in a new situation
Explanation: A "fish out of water" refers to a person in a completely unsuitable environment or situation.

Question 25

The pilot managed to keep his head during the emergency.

  • A. look straight ahead
  • B. remain calm
  • C. shout for help
  • D. save his life
Answer: B. remain calm
Explanation: To "keep one's head" means to remain calm and sensible in a difficult or crisis situation.

SECTION 4: GRAMMAR AND STRUCTURE

Choose the word or group of words that best completes the sentence.

Question 31

Neither the governor nor his deputies ______ present at the ceremony.

  • A. was
  • B. were
  • C. is
  • D. has been
Answer: B. were
Explanation: With "neither/nor", the verb agrees with the closer subject (his deputies), which is plural.

Question 32

John is very good ______ Mathematics.

  • A. in
  • B. at
  • C. with
  • D. for
Answer: B. at
Explanation: The adjective "good" (meaning skillful) is followed by the preposition "at".

Question 33

If I ______ the money, I would have bought that house.

  • A. have
  • B. had
  • C. had had
  • D. have had
Answer: C. had had
Explanation: This is a third conditional (unreal past). It requires "if + past perfect (had had)".

Question 34

The man ______ son won the scholarship is very proud.

  • A. who
  • B. whose
  • C. whom
  • D. which
Answer: B. whose
Explanation: "Whose" is the possessive relative pronoun.

Question 35

The students ______ the classroom before the teacher arrived.

  • A. left
  • B. have left
  • C. had left
  • D. are leaving
Answer: C. had left
Explanation: Use past perfect (had left) for an action completed before another action in the past.

Question 36

She bought a ______ dress for the wedding.

  • A. silk blue beautiful
  • B. beautiful blue silk
  • C. blue beautiful silk
  • D. silk beautiful blue
Answer: B. beautiful blue silk
Explanation: Adjective order: Opinion (beautiful), Color (blue), Material (silk).

Question 37

You are coming to the party, ______?

  • A. are you
  • B. aren't you
  • C. don't you
  • D. you aren't
Answer: B. aren't you
Explanation: A positive statement takes a negative question tag.

Question 38

I am looking ______ my lost keys.

  • A. at
  • B. for
  • C. after
  • D. on
Answer: B. for
Explanation: "Look for" means to search for something.

Note: Questions 39-50 follow the same grammar structure.

Question 39

He hasn't got ______ money in his pocket.

  • A. many
  • B. much
  • C. plenty
  • D. few
Answer: B. much
Explanation: "Much" is used for uncountable nouns like money in negative sentences.

Question 40

The teacher asked us ______ our books to the next class.

  • A. bring
  • B. to bring
  • C. bringing
  • D. brought
Answer: B. to bring
Explanation: The verb "ask" is followed by the object + infinitive (to bring).

Question 41

Not only ______ the window, but he also broke the door.

  • A. he broke
  • B. did he break
  • C. he has broken
  • D. he breaks
Answer: B. did he break
Explanation: When "Not only" begins a sentence, the subject and auxiliary verb must be inverted.

Question 42

This is ______ best film I have ever seen.

  • A. a
  • B. the
  • C. an
  • D. one
Answer: B. the
Explanation: Use the definite article "the" with superlative adjectives like "best".

Question 43

The students enjoy ______ football every Saturday.

  • A. to play
  • B. playing
  • C. play
  • D. played
Answer: B. playing
Explanation: The verb "enjoy" is followed by a gerund (-ing).

Question 44

Everyone ______ to be careful when crossing the road.

  • A. must
  • B. have
  • C. has
  • D. should
Answer: C. has
Explanation: "Everyone" is a singular pronoun and takes the singular verb "has" (in the context of "has to").

Question 45

One of the boys ______ missing from the camp yesterday.

  • A. are
  • B. were
  • C. was
  • D. have been
Answer: C. was
Explanation: The subject "One" is singular, so the verb must be singular "was".

Question 46

This room is ______ than the one we stayed in last year.

  • A. more large
  • B. largest
  • C. larger
  • D. most large
Answer: C. larger
Explanation: The comparative form of "large" is "larger".

Question 47

The cake ______ by my mother for my birthday.

  • A. baked
  • B. was baked
  • C. is baking
  • D. was baking
Answer: B. was baked
Explanation: This is a passive voice sentence.

Question 48

I suggest that he ______ the meeting tomorrow.

  • A. attends
  • B. attend
  • C. should attend
  • D. attended
Answer: B. attend
Explanation: This is the subjunctive mood used after verbs of suggestion; the base form "attend" is used.

Question 49

Hardly ______ the station when the train left.

  • A. reached I
  • B. had I reached
  • C. I reached
  • D. did I reach
Answer: B. had I reached
Explanation: Inversion occurs after negative adverbs like "Hardly".

Question 50

You will not succeed ______ you work very hard.

  • A. if
  • B. unless
  • C. because
  • D. when
Answer: B. unless
Explanation: "Unless" means "if... not", which fits the logic of the sentence.
