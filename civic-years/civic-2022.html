Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

The act of being faithful and committed to the growth of one's community is

  • A. Laziness
  • B. Patriotism
  • C. Selfishness
  • D. Arrogance
Answer: B. Patriotism
Explanation: Patriotism is the love and devotion a citizen has for their country and its progress.

Question 2

A major attribute of a person with integrity is

  • A. Greed
  • B. Trustworthiness
  • C. Fear
  • D. Deceit
Answer: B. Trustworthiness
Explanation: Integrity involves being honest and having strong moral principles that make others trust you.

Question 3

Which of the following values helps in reducing the rate of crime in society?

  • A. Corruption
  • B. Contentment
  • C. Theft
  • D. Bribery
Answer: B. Contentment
Explanation: Contentment makes an individual satisfied with what they have, thereby reducing the urge to steal.

Question 4

Fundamental human rights are called natural rights because they are

  • A. Given by the police
  • B. Inherent in every human being at birth
  • C. Only for the rich
  • D. Bought with money
Answer: B. Inherent in every human being at birth
Explanation: Natural rights are basic freedoms that belong to everyone by virtue of being human.

Question 5

The state of being a legal member of a nation is

  • A. Alienship
  • B. Citizenship
  • C. Ownership
  • D. Partnership
Answer: B. Citizenship
Explanation: Citizenship provides an individual with legal status and protections within a country.

Question 6

A citizen who obeys all the laws of the land is performing a

  • A. Social right
  • B. Civic duty
  • C. Political favor
  • D. Economic right
Answer: B. Civic duty
Explanation: Obeying laws is a compulsory obligation every citizen must perform for society to function.

Question 7

The habit of being orderly and controlled in behavior is

  • A. Disorder
  • B. Discipline
  • C. Violence
  • D. Rebellion
Answer: B. Discipline
Explanation: Discipline involves training oneself to obey rules and act in an organized manner.

Question 8

Which of the following is a symptom of a cultist?

  • A. Academic focus
  • B. Secrecy and strange signs
  • C. Honesty
  • D. Friendliness to all
Answer: B. Secrecy and strange signs
Explanation: Cult members often use secret codes and exhibit suspicious behavior to hide their activities.

Question 9

The use of drugs without a medical expert recommendation is

  • A. Drug trial
  • B. Drug abuse
  • C. Drug sale
  • D. Drug manufacture
Answer: B. Drug abuse
Explanation: Taking drugs in a way that is not prescribed by a doctor is dangerous to health and is termed abuse.

Question 10

The arm of government that carries out the day to day administration of the state is the

  • A. Legislature
  • B. Executive
  • C. Judiciary
  • D. Police
Answer: B. Executive
Explanation: The executive implements the policies and manages the affairs of the nation.

Question 11

A constitution that is written in a single document is a

  • A. Rigid constitution
  • B. Codified constitution
  • C. Flexible constitution
  • D. Federal constitution
Answer: B. Codified constitution
Explanation: A codified constitution is written down in a systematic and organized legal document.

Question 12

Rule of law ensures that

  • A. The rich are not punished
  • B. No one is above the law
  • C. The government can do anything
  • D. Laws apply only to children
Answer: B. No one is above the law
Explanation: Rule of law means that the legal system applies equally to all people regardless of rank.

Question 13

One way to encourage popular participation in politics is through

  • A. Threatening citizens
  • B. Political education
  • C. Rigging elections
  • D. Banning newspapers
Answer: B. Political education
Explanation: Educating the people about their rights and the importance of voting increases participation.

Question 14

  • A. Good governance

Explanation: When leaders do not do what they promised, citizens lose interest in the political process.

Question 15

A democratic government is characterized by

  • A. Use of force
  • B. Respect for human rights

Explanation: Protecting the rights of citizens is a fundamental part of any democratic society.

Question 16
