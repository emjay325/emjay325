Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

A quality of being honest and having strong moral principles is known as

  • A. Contentment
  • B. Integrity
  • C. Discipline
  • D. Fairness
Answer: B. Integrity
Explanation: Integrity involves being truthful and firm in moral values even when no one is watching.

Question 2

The state of being happy and satisfied with what one has is called

  • A. Greed
  • B. Courage
  • C. Contentment
  • D. Selfishness
Answer: C. Contentment
Explanation: Contentment helps individuals avoid social vices like theft and corruption.

Question 3

The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted by the United Nations in the year

  • A. 1945
  • B. 1948
  • C. 1960
  • D. 1999
Answer: B. 1948
Explanation: The document was adopted on December 10 1948 to protect the fundamental rights of all people.

Question 4

Which of the following is a fundamental human right?

  • A. Right to steal
  • B. Right to life
  • C. Right to kill
  • D. Right to jump school
Answer: B. Right to life
Explanation: Every human being has the basic right to exist and be protected from unlawful killing.

Question 5

A person who is a legal member of a country is called a

  • A. Stranger
  • B. Tourist
  • C. Citizen
  • D. Alien
Answer: C. Citizen
Explanation: Citizenship grants an individual legal rights and duties within a specific nation.

Question 6

The method through which a person becomes a citizen of a country by birth is called citizenship by

  • A. Naturalization
  • B. Registration
  • C. Birth
  • D. Honor
Answer: C. Birth
Explanation: This occurs when someone is born within the territory of a country or born to parents who are citizens.

Question 7

Which of the following is an obligation of a citizen?

  • A. Fighting neighbors
  • B. Paying taxes
  • C. Sleeping all day
  • D. Avoiding elections
Answer: B. Paying taxes
Explanation: Citizens have a duty to contribute to the revenue of the state for development.

Question 8

The habit of doing things according to set rules and regulations is

  • A. Laziness
  • B. Discipline
  • C. Arrogance
  • D. Disobedience
Answer: B. Discipline
Explanation: Discipline involves self control and respect for authority and order.

Question 9

Which agency in Nigeria is responsible for fighting the use of fake drugs?

  • A. NDLEA
  • B. NAFDAC
  • C. EFCC
  • D. ICPC
Answer: B. NAFDAC
Explanation: NAFDAC regulates food and drugs to ensure they are safe for public consumption.

Question 10

The illegal act of taking people to other countries for forced labor is known as

  • A. Tourism
  • B. Human trafficking
  • C. Migration
  • D. Citizenship
Answer: B. Human trafficking
Explanation: This is a modern form of slavery that involves exploiting vulnerable people.

Question 11

One way to prevent HIV AIDS is through

  • A. Sharing needles
  • B. Public enlightenment
  • C. Shaking hands
  • D. Using unsterilized tools
Answer: B. Public enlightenment
Explanation: Educating the public on how the virus is spread helps in reducing its transmission.

Question 12

The supreme law of a country is called the

  • A. Manifesto
  • B. Constitution
  • C. Handbook
  • D. Diary
Answer: B. Constitution
Explanation: The constitution provides the framework for governance and outlines the rights of citizens.

Question 13

A government that is ruled by the people and for the people is called

  • A. Monarchy
  • B. Dictatorship
  • C. Democracy
  • D. Oligarchy
Answer: C. Democracy
Explanation: Democracy allows citizens to participate in decision making through elected representatives.

Question 14

The arm of government responsible for making laws is the

  • A. Executive
  • B. Judiciary
  • C. Legislature
  • D. Police
Answer: C. Legislature
Explanation: The legislature debates and passes laws for the good of the society.

Question 15

The arm of government that interprets laws is the

  • A. Executive
  • B. Judiciary
  • C. Legislature
  • D. Military
Answer: B. Judiciary
Explanation: The judiciary consists of the courts and judges who settle disputes based on the law.

Question 16

Rule of law means that

  • A. The President is above the law
  • B. Everyone is equal before the law
  • C. Only the poor should be punished
  • D. Laws are made for only one tribe
Answer: B. Everyone is equal before the law
Explanation: Rule of law ensures that laws apply equally to all citizens regardless of their status.

Question 17

Cultism is a social vice that often leads to

  • A. Academic success
  • B. Peace in school
  • C. Violence and death
  • D. National development
Answer: C. Violence and death
Explanation: Cultism involves secret societies that engage in harmful and criminal activities.

Question 18

Which of the following is a cause of drug abuse?

  • A. Peer pressure
  • B. Proper education
  • C. Good parenting
  • D. Self discipline
Answer: A. Peer pressure
Explanation: Many young people start taking drugs to feel accepted by their friends.

Question 19

The act of working together to achieve a common goal is

  • A. Conflict
  • B. Cooperation
  • C. Isolation
  • D. Competition
Answer: B. Cooperation
Explanation: Cooperation is essential for the growth and development of any community.

Question 20

A set of people who live together in a particular area and share common interests is a

  • A. Crowd
  • B. Community
  • C. Gang
  • D. Audience
Answer: B. Community
Explanation: Communities provide a sense of belonging and support for their members.

Question 21

Public corporations are owned and managed by the

  • A. Private sector
  • B. Government
  • C. Students
  • D. Foreigners
Answer: B. Government
Explanation: These organizations are established by the state to provide essential services to the public.

Question 22

Which of these is a way of promoting national unity?

  • A. Tribalism
  • B. Tolerance
  • C. Religious bias
  • D. Corruption
Answer: B. Tolerance
Explanation: Being tolerant of other people's culture and religion helps a nation stay united.

Question 23

The symbol of authority in the legislature is the

  • A. Flag
  • B. Mace
  • C. Gavel
  • D. Bible
Answer: B. Mace
Explanation: The mace represents the power and authority of the legislative house.

Question 24

Capital punishment refers to the

  • A. Payment of fine
  • B. Death penalty
  • C. Life imprisonment
  • D. Community service
Answer: B. Death penalty
Explanation: This is a legal punishment where a person is sentenced to death for a serious crime.

Question 25

The head of the executive arm of government in Nigeria is the

  • A. Chief Justice
  • B. President
  • C. Senate President
  • D. Governor
Answer: B. President
Explanation: The President serves as the head of state and commander in chief of the armed forces.

Question 26

The process of voting in an election is part of

  • A. Civic responsibility
  • B. Personal gain
  • C. Illegal activity
  • D. Forced labor
Answer: A. Civic responsibility
Explanation: Citizens exercise their power to choose leaders through the voting process.

Question 27

Civil society organizations are important because they

  • A. Support dictatorship
  • B. Promote human rights
  • C. Kill criminals
  • D. Overthrow the government
Answer: B. Promote human rights
Explanation: These non governmental groups work to protect the interests and rights of citizens.

Question 28

Political apathy means

  • A. Love for politics
  • B. Lack of interest in politics
  • C. Joining a political party
  • D. Funding an election
Answer: B. Lack of interest in politics
Explanation: Apathy occurs when citizens refuse to participate in elections or political activities.

Question 29

The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency is mainly responsible for

  • A. Giving free drugs
  • B. Arresting drug traffickers
  • C. Building hospitals
  • D. Teaching in schools
Answer: B. Arresting drug traffickers
Explanation: NDLEA works to stop the cultivation and trade of illegal narcotic drugs.

Question 30

The term HIV stands for

  • A. Human Immune Virus
  • B. Human Immunodeficiency Virus
  • C. Healthy Internal Virus
  • D. High Immune Virus
Answer: B. Human Immunodeficiency Virus
Explanation: It is the virus that weakens the immune system and can lead to AIDS.

Question 31

Universal suffrage means the right of all qualified adults to

  • A. Work
  • B. Vote
  • C. Travel
  • D. Dance
Answer: B. Vote
Explanation: It ensures that every eligible citizen has a voice in choosing their government.

Question 32

The 1999 Constitution of Nigeria was established during the transition to

  • A. Military rule
  • B. Civilian rule
  • C. Colonial rule
  • D. Monarchy
Answer: B. Civilian rule
Explanation: It marked the beginning of the Fourth Republic in Nigeria.

Question 33

Which of the following describes a person with integrity?

  • A. He tells lies
  • B. He is consistent and reliable
  • C. He steals public money
  • D. He cheats in exams
Answer: B. He is consistent and reliable
Explanation: A person of integrity is trustworthy and follows through on their promises.

Question 34

Traffic regulations are made to ensure

  • A. Road accidents
  • B. Orderly flow of traffic
  • C. Chaos on the road
  • D. High speed driving
Answer: B. Orderly flow of traffic
Explanation: Traffic laws protect lives and property by regulating the behavior of road users.

Question 35

A major cause of human trafficking is

  • A. Wealth
  • B. Poverty
  • C. High education
  • D. Family peace
Answer: B. Poverty
Explanation: Traffickers exploit people who are desperately seeking a better life abroad.

Question 36

Which of the following is a symptom of HIV AIDS?

  • A. Growing taller
  • B. Persistent fever and weight loss
  • C. Stronger muscles
  • D. Better vision
Answer: B. Persistent fever and weight loss
Explanation: As the immune system weakens, the body becomes vulnerable to various infections.

Question 37

Civic values are important because they help in

  • A. Creating division
  • B. Building a stable society
  • C. Encouraging crime
  • D. Promoting selfishness
Answer: B. Building a stable society
Explanation: Shared values like respect and justice hold a nation together.

Question 38

The highest court in Nigeria is the

  • A. High Court
  • B. Supreme Court
  • C. Customary Court
  • D. Magistrate Court
Answer: B. Supreme Court
Explanation: The Supreme Court is the final court of appeal and its decisions are binding on all others.

Question 39

The total way of life of a group of people is their

  • A. Policy
  • B. Culture
  • C. Geography
  • D. History
Answer: B. Culture
Explanation: Culture includes the language, food, dress, and beliefs of a people.

Question 40

Which of the following is a characteristic of democracy?

  • A. One party system
  • B. Periodic elections
  • C. Life presidency
  • D. Suppression of the press
Answer: B. Periodic elections
Explanation: Regular elections allow the people to renew or change their leadership.

Question 41

One consequence of drug abuse is

  • A. Improved health
  • B. Mental illness
  • C. Better memory
  • D. Social respect
Answer: B. Mental illness
Explanation: Excessive drug use can damage the brain and lead to permanent psychological disorders.

Question 42

To curb cultism in schools, the government should

  • A. Give them more money
  • B. Enforce strict laws
  • C. Close all schools
  • D. Join them
Answer: B. Enforce strict laws
Explanation: Strict penalties and better security help in discouraging students from joining cults.

Question 43

A person who sees a crime being committed and reports to the police is being

  • A. A gossip
  • B. A patriotic citizen
  • C. A criminal
  • D. A coward
Answer: B. A patriotic citizen
Explanation: Assisting security agencies is part of a citizen's duty to protect the country.

Question 44

Which body is responsible for conducting elections in Nigeria?

  • A. EFCC
  • B. INEC
  • C. NAFDAC
  • D. ICPC
Answer: B. INEC
Explanation: The Independent National Electoral Commission organizes and supervises all elections.

Question 45

Fundamental human rights are contained in the

  • A. Textbook
  • B. Constitution
  • C. Novel
  • D. Newspaper
Answer: B. Constitution
Explanation: The constitution provides legal backing and protection for the rights of all citizens.

Question 46

Stigmatization of HIV AIDS victims is wrong because it

  • A. Cures the disease
  • B. Discourages victims from seeking help
  • C. Makes them happy
  • D. Is a legal requirement
Answer: B. Discourages victims from seeking help
Explanation: Victims who are treated badly may hide their status and spread the virus further.

Question 47

The term rule of law was popularized by

  • A. Adam Smith
  • B. A V Dicey
  • C. Karl Marx
  • D. Abraham Lincoln
Answer: B. A V Dicey
Explanation: He outlined the three main pillars of the rule of law in his legal studies.

Question 48

Which of these is a social problem in Nigeria?

  • A. Overproduction of food
  • B. Corruption
  • C. Too much peace
  • D. High literacy rate
Answer: B. Corruption
Explanation: Corruption hinders development and prevents resources from reaching the poor.

Question 49

Inter ethnic marriage is a way of promoting

  • A. Conflict
  • B. National integration
  • C. Tribalism
  • D. Division
Answer: B. National integration
Explanation: Marrying from different tribes helps people understand and respect other cultures.

Question 50

A system of government where power is shared between the central and state governments is

  • A. Unitary
  • B. Federalism
  • C. Monarchy
  • D. Confederacy
Answer: B. Federalism
Explanation: Federalism allows different levels of government to have authority over specific matters.
