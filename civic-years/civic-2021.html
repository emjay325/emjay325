Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

The act of standing for what is right even when you are alone is an example of

  • A. Physical courage
  • B. Moral courage
  • C. Selfishness
  • D. Tribalism
Answer: B. Moral courage
Explanation: Moral courage involves the internal strength to do what is ethical despite social pressure or personal risk.

Question 2

Which of the following describes a person with a high sense of integrity?

  • A. He takes bribes to do his work
  • B. He is truthful and reliable
  • C. He tells lies to cover his mistakes
  • D. He cheats during national elections
Answer: B. He is truthful and reliable
Explanation: Integrity is the quality of being honest and having strong moral principles.

Question 3

A citizen who pays his water and electricity bills regularly is showing

  • A. Civic responsibility
  • B. Personal pride
  • C. Selfishness
  • D. Fear of the police
Answer: A. Civic responsibility
Explanation: Paying for public utilities ensures the government has funds to maintain essential services.

Question 4

The status granted to a person who is born in a country or whose parents are citizens of that country is

  • A. Naturalization
  • B. Citizenship
  • C. Registration
  • D. Alienation
Answer: B. Citizenship
Explanation: Citizenship is the legal link between an individual and a state.

Question 5

The term used for the rules and regulations that guide the conduct of people in a country is

  • A. Tradition
  • B. Law
  • C. Advice
  • D. News
Answer: B. Law
Explanation: Laws are established to maintain order and protect the rights of every person in the society.

Question 6

A major cause of the spread of HIV in many communities is

  • A. Using a public toilet
  • B. Use of unsterilized sharp objects
  • C. Eating from the same plate
  • D. Living in the same house
Answer: B. Use of unsterilized sharp objects
Explanation: The virus can be transmitted when infected blood on an object enters another person's body.

Question 7

Which of the following is an attribute of discipline?

  • A. Coming to work late
  • B. Self control
  • C. Arrogance
  • D. Breaking rules
Answer: B. Self control
Explanation: Discipline involves the ability to manage one's behavior and follow set guidelines.

Question 8

Which agency in Nigeria is responsible for tracking money laundering and financial crimes?

  • A. NAFDAC
  • B. EFCC
  • C. FRSC
  • D. NAPTIP
Answer: B. EFCC
Explanation: The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission handles cases involving fraud and money laundering.

Question 9

Which of these is a way through which a citizen can participate in the government of his country?

  • A. Avoiding taxes
  • B. Voting in elections
  • C. Rioting
  • D. Staying at home during census
Answer: B. Voting in elections
Explanation: Voting allows citizens to have a say in who leads and manages the country.

Question 10

The act of being satisfied with what one has and not being greedy is

  • A. Poverty
  • B. Contentment
  • C. Laziness
  • D. Pride
Answer: B. Contentment
Explanation: Contentment helps to reduce the temptation to commit crimes like theft or bribery.

Question 11

A society where there is no law and order is in a state of

  • A. Democracy
  • B. Anarchy
  • C. Monarchy
  • D. Federalism
Answer: B. Anarchy
Explanation: Anarchy is a situation where people ignore authority and do as they please, leading to chaos.

Question 12

The arm of government that is in charge of formulating and implementing policies is the

  • A. Judiciary
  • B. Executive
  • C. Legislature
  • D. Civil society
Answer: B. Executive
Explanation: The executive branch carries out the laws and manages the daily affairs of the country.

Question 13

To acquire citizenship through naturalization, an alien must have lived in the country for a period of

  • A. One month
  • B. Several years as specified by law
  • C. Two weeks
  • D. One hundred years
Answer: B. Several years as specified by law
Explanation: Most countries require a minimum period of residency to ensure the person has adapted to the society.

Question 14

The main purpose of the rule of law is to

  • A. Give power to the rich
  • B. Protect the rights of citizens
  • C. Make the president a king
  • D. Abolish the courts
Answer: B. Protect the rights of citizens
Explanation: Rule of law ensures that every person is equal before the legal system and is protected from abuse.

Question 15

Human trafficking is often done through

  • A. Honest job offers
  • B. Deception and fake promises
  • C. Public advertisements
  • D. Official government letters
Answer: B. Deception and fake promises
Explanation: Traffickers lure victims with false promises of better lives or education abroad.

Question 16

Which of the following can lead to political apathy?

  • A. Free and fair elections
  • B. Violence and rigging during elections
  • C. Good governance
  • D. Transparency in government
Answer: B. Violence and rigging during elections
Explanation: When citizens feel their votes do not count or fear for their lives, they stop participating in politics.

Question 17

The document that contains the fundamental human rights of all people in the world is the

  • A. National Diary
  • B. Universal Declaration of Human Rights
  • C. School Handbook
  • D. Business Journal
Answer: B. Universal Declaration of Human Rights
Explanation: Adopted by the United Nations, this document sets the global standard for the protection of human rights.

Question 18

A person who uses his position of authority to favor his relatives is practicing

  • A. Justice
  • B. Nepotism
  • C. Patriotism
  • D. Fairness
Answer: B. Nepotism
Explanation: Nepotism is a form of corruption where family members are favored for jobs or benefits regardless of merit.

Question 19

Which of these describes a democratic society?

  • A. People have no right to speak
  • B. Power belongs to the people
  • C. The king makes all the laws
  • D. Elections are never held
Answer: B. Power belongs to the people
Explanation: In a democracy, the people exercise their power through voting and participating in government.

Question 20

Which agency is responsible for testing and certifying products before they are sold in the market?

  • A. INEC
  • B. SON
  • C. EFCC
  • D. NAPTIP
Answer: B. SON
Explanation: The Standards Organisation of Nigeria ensures that manufactured goods meet the required quality levels.

Question 21

Cultism in higher institutions can be reduced through

  • A. Encouraging students to fight
  • B. Strengthening guidance and counseling services
  • C. Hiding the names of cult members
  • D. Abolishing all student unions
Answer: B. Strengthening guidance and counseling services
Explanation: Helping students understand the dangers of cultism and providing support can prevent them from joining.

Question 22

A person who is loyal to his country and works for its peace and unity is a

  • A. Criminal
  • B. Patriot
  • C. Stranger
  • D. Traitor
Answer: B. Patriot
Explanation: Patriotism is the love and commitment to the success and safety of one's own nation.

Question 23

Public service should be characterized by

  • A. Secrecy
  • B. Accountability and transparency
  • C. Corruption
  • D. Favoritism
Answer: B. Accountability and transparency
Explanation: Public officials must be open about their work and responsible for how they use public resources.

Question 24

One of the functions of the judiciary is to

  • A. Make laws
  • B. Interpret and apply the law
  • C. Build roads
  • D. Print money
Answer: B. Interpret and apply the law
Explanation: The judiciary ensures that the law is followed and settles disputes in the society.

Question 25

To promote national unity, we should encourage

  • A. Inter ethnic interaction and marriage
  • B. Discrimination
  • C. Religious war
  • D. Tribal politics
Answer: A. Inter ethnic interaction and marriage
Explanation: Interaction between different ethnic groups helps to build understanding and reduce tribalism.

Question 26

The term HIV stands for

  • A. High Immune Virus
  • B. Human Immunodeficiency Virus
  • C. Healthy Internal Virus
  • D. Human Indirect Vessel
Answer: B. Human Immunodeficiency Virus
Explanation: This is the specific name of the virus that leads to the breakdown of the immune system.

Question 27

An individual who has attained the age of 18 in Nigeria has the right to

  • A. Steal
  • B. Vote and be voted for
  • C. Break traffic laws
  • D. Use hard drugs
Answer: B. Vote and be voted for
Explanation: 18 is the legal voting age in Nigeria, allowing young adults to participate in democratic choices.

Question 28

A person who uses his skills to work for himself and not for a company is being

  • A. Self reliant
  • B. Greedy
  • C. Lazy
  • D. Dependent
Answer: A. Self reliant
Explanation: Self reliance is the ability to provide for oneself through personal skills and hard work.

Question 29

What is the effect of stigmatization on someone living with HIV AIDS?

  • A. It cures the person
  • B. It leads to depression and isolation
  • C. It makes the person famous
  • D. It improves the health of the person
Answer: B. It leads to depression and isolation
Explanation: Stigma makes the person feel rejected by the society, which can worsen their mental and physical health.

Question 30

The constitution of a country is the supreme law. This means

  • A. It can be ignored by the president
  • B. Every other law must follow it
  • C. It is only for the police
  • D. It changes every week
Answer: B. Every other law must follow it
Explanation: Any law that goes against the constitution is considered invalid.

Question 31

Which of the following is a duty of a citizen during a national census?

  • A. To hide from enumerators
  • B. To provide accurate information
  • C. To give false names
  • D. To demand money for being counted
Answer: B. To provide accurate information
Explanation: Census data helps the government to plan for schools, hospitals, and other infrastructure.

Question 32

The symbols used to identify a country and its identity are

  • A. Private cars
  • B. National symbols
  • C. Business cards
  • D. School books
Answer: B. National symbols
Explanation: Symbols like the flag, national anthem, and coat of arms represent the pride of a nation.

Question 33

To reduce corruption in Nigeria, the government should

  • A. Increase the salary of only politicians
  • B. Enforce strict laws and penalties
  • C. Abolish all courts
  • D. Allow anyone to take public money
Answer: B. Enforce strict laws and penalties
Explanation: When people know they will be punished for being corrupt, they are less likely to do it.

Question 34

The act of working with others to keep the community clean is called

  • A. Communal labor
  • B. Individual work
  • C. Selfish act
  • D. Personal task
Answer: A. Communal labor
Explanation: Communal labor fosters unity and helps to achieve goals that benefit the whole community.

Question 35

A responsible citizen should protect public property by

  • A. Breaking street lights
  • B. Reporting vandals to the authorities
  • C. Stealing electricity cables
  • D. Damaging government cars
Answer: B. Reporting vandals to the authorities
Explanation: Protecting public property ensures that facilities remain available for everyone to use.

Question 36

Which of the following describes a rigid constitution?

  • A. It can be changed easily
  • B. It requires a difficult procedure to amend
  • C. It is never written down
  • D. It is only for one tribe
Answer: B. It requires a difficult procedure to amend
Explanation: Rigid constitutions are designed to prevent frequent and unnecessary changes to the supreme law.

Question 37

Fundamental human rights can be limited when a person

  • A. Goes to church
  • B. Commits a criminal offence
  • C. Eats food
  • D. Reads a book
Answer: B. Commits a criminal offence
Explanation: For instance, a person's right to movement is restricted if they are legally sent to prison for a crime.

Question 38

One of the duties of a student in the school is to

  • A. Fight with fellow students
  • B. Respect school rules and authorities
  • C. Skip classes
  • D. Destroy school windows
Answer: B. Respect school rules and authorities
Explanation: Following rules helps to create an environment where everyone can learn peacefully.

Question 39

Civic values are important because they lead to

  • A. Violence
  • B. Social order and stability
  • C. Tribalism
  • D. Corruption
Answer: B. Social order and stability
Explanation: Values like respect, honesty, and justice help the society to function properly.

Question 40

A person who sells fake and expired drugs is committing an offence against

  • A. Only his family
  • B. The health and safety of the public
  • C. Only himself
  • D. His neighbor only
Answer: B. The health and safety of the public
Explanation: Fake drugs can cause serious illness or death, and agencies like NAFDAC work to stop this.

Question 41

What is the main role of the legislature?

  • A. To arrest criminals
  • B. To make laws for the good of the state
  • C. To settle cases in court
  • D. To implement government policies
Answer: B. To make laws for the good of the state
Explanation: The legislature debates and passes bills which become laws to guide the nation.

Question 42

A major consequence of drug abuse is

  • A. Success in exams
  • B. Mental health disorders
  • C. Strong physical health
  • D. Good reputation
Answer: B. Mental health disorders
Explanation: Constant use of illegal drugs can damage the brain and cause psychological problems.

Question 43

Which of the following is a symptom of a person living with AIDS?

  • A. Persistent skin infections and weight loss
  • B. Getting taller and stronger
  • C. Having a very bright skin
  • D. Improved vision
Answer: A. Persistent skin infections and weight loss
Explanation: AIDS weakens the body so much that it cannot fight off common infections.

Question 44

The ability of citizens to choose their leaders periodically through elections is a feature of

  • A. Dictatorship
  • B. Democracy
  • C. Totalitarianism
  • D. Anarchy
Answer: B. Democracy
Explanation: Regular elections allow people to change or renew their trust in their leaders.

Question 45

Which body is in charge of protecting the environment in Nigeria?

  • A. INEC
  • B. NESREA
  • C. EFCC
  • D. NAFDAC
Answer: B. NESREA
Explanation: This agency ensures that environmental laws and standards are followed to prevent pollution and degradation.

Question 46

A good leader should be ready to

  • A. Steal public money
  • B. Serve the interest of the people
  • C. Encourage tribalism
  • D. Ignore the law
Answer: B. Serve the interest of the people
Explanation: Leadership is about service and improving the lives of the members of the society.

Question 47

The state of being honest and unbiased in decision making is

  • A. Favoritism
  • B. Justice and fairness
  • C. Nepotism
  • D. Corruption
Answer: B. Justice and fairness
Explanation: These values ensure that everyone gets what they deserve according to the law.

Question 48

One way to improve the quality of education in Nigeria is through

  • A. Encouraging examination malpractice
  • B. Proper funding and training of teachers
  • C. Closing all schools
  • D. Reducing the hours of study to zero
Answer: B. Proper funding and training of teachers
Explanation: Good resources and skilled teachers lead to better learning outcomes for students.

Question 49

What is the consequence of being a cultist?

  • A. Winning a scholarship
  • B. Expulsion from school and legal punishment
  • C. Becoming the most popular student
  • D. Getting a high paying job easily
Answer: B. Expulsion from school and legal punishment
Explanation: Schools and the government have zero tolerance for cultism due to its violent nature.

Question 50

Civic responsibility means that a citizen should be

  • A. Ignorant of the law
  • B. Involved in the affairs of his community
  • C. Selfish with his time
  • D. Violent during protests
Answer: B. Involved in the affairs of his community
Explanation: Active participation helps in identifying and solving problems in the society.
