Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

The ability to do what is right even in the face of danger is called

  • A. Cowardice
  • B. Courage
  • C. Selfishness
  • D. Pride
Answer: B. Courage
Explanation: Courage allows a person to stand for the truth and perform their duties despite threats or fears.

Question 2

A person who depends on his own abilities and resources rather than others is said to be

  • A. Self centered
  • B. Self reliant
  • C. Self important
  • D. Selfish
Answer: B. Self reliant
Explanation: Self reliance is a value that encourages individuals to use their skills to provide for themselves and contribute to society.

Question 3

Citizenship acquired through marriage is known as citizenship by

  • A. Birth
  • B. Registration
  • C. Naturalization
  • D. Honor
Answer: B. Registration
Explanation: In many countries, a person can apply to become a citizen by being legally married to a citizen.

Question 4

Which of the following is a way through which human rights can be protected?

  • A. Military rule
  • B. Independent judiciary
  • C. Police brutality
  • D. Censorship of the press
Answer: B. Independent judiciary
Explanation: When the courts are free from government control, they can fairly judge cases involving the violation of rights.

Question 5

The first ten amendments to the United States Constitution that protect individual rights are called the

  • A. Bill of Rights
  • B. Magna Carta
  • C. Social Contract
  • D. People Charter
Answer: A. Bill of Rights
Explanation: These amendments were designed to guarantee essential freedoms and legal protections.

Question 6

The practice of having more than one citizenship is known as

  • A. Single citizenship
  • B. Dual citizenship
  • C. International citizenship
  • D. Multiple residence
Answer: B. Dual citizenship
Explanation: Some countries allow individuals to hold the legal status of a citizen in two different nations at once.

Question 7

Which of the following is an example of an economic right?

  • A. Right to vote
  • B. Right to work and earn a living
  • C. Right to life
  • D. Right to fair hearing
Answer: B. Right to work and earn a living
Explanation: Economic rights are those that allow a person to participate in the financial and labor activities of a nation.

Question 8

The agency in Nigeria responsible for the control of hard drugs like cocaine is

  • A. NAFDAC
  • B. NDLEA
  • C. EFCC
  • D. ICPC
Answer: B. NDLEA
Explanation: The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency is tasked with eliminating the processing and sale of narcotic drugs.

Question 9

A primary cause of examination malpractice among students is

  • A. Hard work
  • B. Poor preparation
  • C. High intelligence
  • D. Honesty
Answer: B. Poor preparation
Explanation: Students who fail to study often resort to cheating to pass their exams.

Question 10

Which of the following describes the behavior of a disciplined citizen?

  • A. Coming late to work
  • B. Obeying traffic rules
  • C. Fighting in public
  • D. Vandalizing public property
Answer: B. Obeying traffic rules
Explanation: Discipline involves self control and the willingness to follow established laws and regulations.

Question 11

Human trafficking can lead to the spread of diseases like

  • A. Malaria
  • B. HIV AIDS
  • C. Cough
  • D. Headache
Answer: B. HIV AIDS
Explanation: Victims of trafficking are often subjected to sexual abuse which increases the risk of spreading infections.

Question 12

The act of being satisfied with one's possessions and avoiding greed is

  • A. Selfishness
  • B. Contentment
  • C. Arrogance
  • D. Wealth
Answer: B. Contentment
Explanation: Contentment promotes peace of mind and reduces the temptation to engage in criminal acts for money.

Question 13

Which of the following can prevent communal conflicts?

  • A. Hatred
  • B. Tolerance and dialogue
  • C. Religious bias
  • D. Land grabbing
Answer: B. Tolerance and dialogue
Explanation: Communities can avoid violence by talking through their differences and respecting each other.

Question 14

The document that contains the fundamental rules for governing a state is the

  • A. Manifesto
  • B. Constitution
  • C. Decree
  • D. Journal
Answer: B. Constitution
Explanation: The constitution is the supreme law that defines the power of government and the rights of the governed.

Question 15

One of the duties of a citizen during an election is to

  • A. Steal ballot boxes
  • B. Vote peacefully
  • C. Campaign for thugs
  • D. Stay at home and sleep
Answer: B. Vote peacefully
Explanation: It is a civic responsibility for eligible adults to cast their votes to choose their leaders.

Question 16

The arm of government that settles legal disputes is the

  • A. Executive
  • B. Judiciary
  • C. Legislature
  • D. Press
Answer: B. Judiciary
Explanation: The judiciary ensures that justice is served by interpreting laws and punishing offenders.

Question 17

A society where laws are strictly followed and people are safe is characterized by

  • A. Anarchy
  • B. Orderliness
  • C. Chaos
  • D. Tyranny
Answer: B. Orderliness
Explanation: Orderliness helps in maintaining a peaceful environment for social and economic growth.

Question 18

The practice of taking drugs for purposes other than medical reasons is

  • A. Drug prescription
  • B. Drug abuse
  • C. Drug marketing
  • D. Drug trial
Answer: B. Drug abuse
Explanation: Drug abuse can lead to addiction and serious health complications for the user.

Question 19

Which of the following agency prevents the sale of expired canned food?

  • A. NDLEA
  • B. NAFDAC
  • C. EFCC
  • D. ICPC
Answer: B. NAFDAC
Explanation: NAFDAC ensures that all processed foods and drugs are safe and within their shelf life.

Question 20

The term HIV AIDS affects the body by

  • A. Improving vision
  • B. Weakening the immune system
  • C. Strengthening bones
  • D. Increasing energy
Answer: B. Weakening the immune system
Explanation: The virus attacks the white blood cells making the body unable to fight simple infections.

Question 21

A government where power is concentrated in the hands of a single person is

  • A. Democracy
  • B. Autocracy
  • C. Federalism
  • D. Republic
Answer: B. Autocracy
Explanation: In an autocracy or dictatorship, the ruler has absolute power and the people have little or no say.

Question 22

Which of the following is a symptom of political apathy?

  • A. Massive turnout during elections
  • B. Refusal to register for voting
  • C. Joining a political party
  • D. Donating to campaign funds
Answer: B. Refusal to register for voting
Explanation: Political apathy is characterized by indifference and lack of interest in the political process.

Question 23

Human rights are said to be inalienable because they

  • A. Can be bought
  • B. Cannot be taken away from an individual
  • C. Are only for the rich
  • D. Change every year
Answer: B. Cannot be taken away from an individual
Explanation: These rights are natural to every human and should not be denied by any authority.

Question 24

The agency that handles trafficking in persons NAPTIP is a agency of the

  • A. School
  • B. Government
  • C. Church
  • D. Family
Answer: B. Government
Explanation: NAPTIP is a specialized government agency created to fight human trafficking and domestic servitude.

Question 25

Cultism can lead to the expulsion of a student from school. This is a

  • A. Positive result
  • B. Negative consequence
  • C. High honor
  • D. Scholarship opportunity
Answer: B. Negative consequence
Explanation: Cultism destroys the academic career and future of those who participate in it.

Question 26

Which of the following describes the rule of law?

  • A. Only the poor must follow the law
  • B. The law is supreme and applies to everyone
  • C. The King can do no wrong
  • D. Laws can be changed at any time by the rich
Answer: B. The law is supreme and applies to everyone
Explanation: Rule of law means that no one is above the legal system, including leaders.

Question 27

Nationalism is important for a country because it

  • A. Causes war between tribes
  • B. Promotes unity and national interest
  • C. Helps in stealing public funds
  • D. Encourages tribalism
Answer: B. Promotes unity and national interest
Explanation: A nationalist person puts the needs of their country above their personal or ethnic desires.

Question 28

The primary duty of the executive arm of government is to

  • A. Make laws
  • B. Execute or implement laws
  • C. Judge criminals
  • D. Control the press
Answer: B. Execute or implement laws
Explanation: The executive ensures that the laws passed by the legislature are carried out through various agencies.

Question 29

One way to improve self reliance is through

  • A. Depending on parents forever
  • B. Skill acquisition and vocational training
  • C. Begging for food
  • D. Watching television all day
Answer: B. Skill acquisition and vocational training
Explanation: Learning a trade or skill makes an individual capable of earning their own income.

Question 30

Which of the following is a civic responsibility of a citizen?

  • A. Reporting for duty late
  • B. Participating in community development
  • C. Discouraging people from paying tax
  • D. Fighting for one's tribe only
Answer: B. Participating in community development
Explanation: Citizens should contribute their time and effort to improve the environment where they live.

Question 31

An individual who is loyal to his nation and respects its symbols is a

  • A. Rebel
  • B. Patriot
  • C. Stranger
  • D. Politician
Answer: B. Patriot
Explanation: Patriotism involves love and commitment to the progress and well being of one's country.

Question 32

Public service includes all the following except

  • A. Teaching in a government school
  • B. Working for a secret cult
  • C. Serving in the Nigerian Army
  • D. Working in a state hospital
Answer: B. Working for a secret cult
Explanation: Public service refers to legitimate work done for the state to provide social services.

Question 33

Democracy is best defined as the government of the

  • A. Military
  • B. People
  • C. Foreigners
  • D. Rich
Answer: B. People
Explanation: Democracy originates from the Greek words meaning the people rule.

Question 34

The act of following a set of moral principles and being truthful is

  • A. Greed
  • B. Integrity
  • C. Pride
  • D. Corruption
Answer: B. Integrity
Explanation: Integrity is a core civic value that involves honesty and strong character.

Question 35

Which of the following can lead to the loss of citizenship?

  • A. Voting in an election
  • B. Committing an act of treason
  • C. Traveling abroad
  • D. Marrying a foreigner
Answer: B. Committing an act of treason
Explanation: Treason involves betraying one's country and is a serious ground for revoking legal membership.

Question 36

Civil society helps to check the excess of government through

  • A. Carrying guns
  • B. Public protests and advocacy
  • C. Supporting illegal laws
  • D. Hiding information
Answer: B. Public protests and advocacy
Explanation: Non governmental groups use peaceful means to voice the concerns of the people to the leaders.

Question 37

Road signs are placed on the highways to

  • A. Beautify the environment
  • B. Guide and warn road users
  • C. Cause confusion
  • D. Increase speed
Answer: B. Guide and warn road users
Explanation: Road signs are essential for traffic safety and prevent collisions and errors by drivers.

Question 38

The use of unsterilized needles can lead to the transmission of

  • A. Flu
  • B. HIV AIDS
  • C. Cholera
  • D. Polio
Answer: B. HIV AIDS
Explanation: Sharing sharp objects with an infected person allows the virus to enter another person's bloodstream.

Question 39

In a federal system of government power is shared between

  • A. Only the central and state governments
  • B. All levels of government defined by the constitution
  • C. The President and his family
  • D. The police and the army only
Answer: B. All levels of government defined by the constitution
Explanation: Federalism involves sharing authority to ensure development reaches every part of the country.

Question 40

A person who treats everyone equally regardless of tribe is being

  • A. Biased
  • B. Just and fair
  • C. Selfish
  • D. Tribalistic
Answer: B. Just and fair
Explanation: Justice and fairness are fundamental civic values that promote equality in society.

Question 41

The term for a government that controls all aspects of life is

  • A. Democratic
  • B. Totalitarian
  • C. Liberal
  • D. Representative
Answer: B. Totalitarian
Explanation: Totalitarian regimes do not allow for individual freedom and use strict control over the public.

Question 42

Which of the following is a consequence of drug trafficking?

  • A. Economic growth
  • B. Destruction of national image
  • C. Good relationship with other nations
  • D. High employment rate
Answer: B. Destruction of national image
Explanation: Nations whose citizens are known for drug trade face harsh treatment and travel restrictions.

Question 43

Participation in politics is a right that is usually granted to

  • A. Only foreigners
  • B. Only citizens of a country
  • C. Only children
  • D. Only the military
Answer: B. Only citizens of a country
Explanation: Citizenship brings with it the legal right to vote and hold public office.

Question 44

The Independent National Electoral Commission INEC was created to

  • A. Collect taxes
  • B. Organize and supervise elections
  • C. Build roads
  • D. Fight drug abuse
Answer: B. Organize and supervise elections
Explanation: INEC is the body in Nigeria mandated to conduct free and fair elections.

Question 45

Which of the following body protects the rights of women and children in Nigeria?

  • A. Ministry of Finance
  • B. Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development
  • C. Central Bank
  • D. Ministry of Education
Answer: B. Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development
Explanation: This ministry formulates policies to empower and protect vulnerable members of society.

Question 46

Popular participation in democracy can be encouraged through

  • A. Rigging elections
  • B. Civic education and awareness
  • C. Threatening voters
  • D. Using money to buy votes
Answer: B. Civic education and awareness
Explanation: When people know the importance of their participation they are more likely to get involved.

Question 47

Which of these is a way of promoting national unity?

  • A. Promoting ethnic dominance
  • B. Creating national symbols like the flag
  • C. Favoring only one religion
  • D. Fighting other tribes
Answer: B. Creating national symbols like the flag
Explanation: National symbols represent the entire nation and help build a sense of common identity.

Question 48

A person who does not allow others to express their opinion is being

  • A. Democratic
  • B. Intolerant
  • C. Patriotic
  • D. Disciplined
Answer: B. Intolerant
Explanation: Intolerance leads to conflict and is contrary to the democratic principle of freedom of speech.

Question 49

Universal suffrage means that the right to vote is given to

  • A. Only rich men
  • B. All eligible adult citizens
  • C. Only religious leaders
  • D. Only members of the ruling party
Answer: B. All eligible adult citizens
Explanation: Universal suffrage ensures that everyone has an equal voice in governance.

Question 50

National integrity involves a country being

  • A. Divided and weak
  • B. Whole and incorruptible
  • C. Dependent on foreign aid only
  • D. Ruled by the military forever
Answer: B. Whole and incorruptible
Explanation: National integrity means the state is functioning properly and its institutions are honest and effective.
