Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

Convert 101101 (base 2) to a number in base 10.

  • A. 45
  • B. 40
  • C. 35
  • D. 50
Answer: A. 45
Explanation: (1x32) + (0x16) + (1x8) + (1x4) + (0x2) + (1x1) = 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45.

Question 2

If 2/3 of a number is added to 1/4 of the same number, the result is 22. Find the number.

  • A. 12
  • B. 24
  • C. 36
  • D. 48
Answer: B. 24
Explanation: (2x/3) + (x/4) = 22. Find LCM (12): (8x + 3x)/12 = 22 -> 11x = 264 -> x = 24.

Question 3

The sum of the interior angles of a regular polygon is 1080°. How many sides does the polygon have?

  • A. 6
  • B. 7
  • C. 8
  • D. 10
Answer: C. 8
Explanation: (n - 2) x 180 = 1080. Divide by 180: n - 2 = 6. Therefore, n = 8.

Question 4

Simplify: √75 - √27 + √12

  • A. 4√3
  • B. 3√3
  • C. 10√3
  • D. 2√3
Answer: A. 4√3
Explanation: 5√3 - 3√3 + 2√3 = (5 - 3 + 2)√3 = 4√3.

Question 5

A bag contains 5 red, 4 blue, and 6 yellow sweets. What is the probability of picking a blue sweet at random?

  • A. 1/3
  • B. 4/15
  • C. 2/5
  • D. 1/15
Answer: B. 4/15
Explanation: Total = 5 + 4 + 6 = 15. Probability = Blue / Total = 4/15.

Question 6

Solve the inequality: 3(x - 2) < 2x + 5

  • A. x < 11
  • B. x > 11
  • C. x < 1
  • D. x > -1
Answer: A. x < 11
Explanation: 3x - 6 < 2x + 5. Subtract 2x from both sides: x - 6 < 5. Add 6: x < 11.

Question 7

Find the value of x if log(base 10) x = -2

  • A. 20
  • B. 0.2
  • C. 0.01
  • D. 0.02
Answer: C. 0.01
Explanation: x = 10 raised to the power of -2. x = 1/100 = 0.01.

Question 8

The bearing of P from Q is 065°. What is the bearing of Q from P?

  • A. 115°
  • B. 225°
  • C. 245°
  • D. 295°
Answer: C. 245°
Explanation: Back bearing = 180° + 65° = 245°.

Question 9

Factorize completely: 4x² - 9y²

  • A. (2x - 3y)(2x - 3y)
  • B. (2x + 3y)(2x - 3y)
  • C. (4x - 9y)(x + y)
  • D. (2x + 9y)(2x - y)
Answer: B. (2x + 3y)(2x - 3y)
Explanation: This is a difference of two squares: (2x)² - (3y)².

Question 10

Calculate the simple interest on N20,000 for 3 years at 5% per annum.

  • A. N3,000
  • B. N4,000
  • C. N2,000
  • D. N3,500
Answer: A. N3,000
Explanation: Interest = (Principal x Rate x Time) / 100 = (20000 x 5 x 3) / 100 = 3000.

Question 11

If y varies inversely as x, and y = 10 when x = 2, find y when x = 5.

  • A. 25
  • B. 4
  • C. 1
  • D. 20
Answer: B. 4
Explanation: y = k/x. So, 10 = k/2 -> k = 20. Then y = 20/5 = 4.

Question 12

Find the gradient of the line joining points A(2, 5) and B(4, 9).

  • A. 1/2
  • B. 2
  • C. 3
  • D. 4
Answer: B. 2
Explanation: Gradient (m) = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1) = (9 - 5) / (4 - 2) = 4 / 2 = 2.

Question 13

The mean of 5, 8, 10, x, and 12 is 9. Find the value of x.

  • A. 8
  • B. 9
  • C. 10
  • D. 11
Answer: C. 10
Explanation: (5 + 8 + 10 + x + 12) / 5 = 9. Total = 35 + x = 45. x = 10.

Question 14

What is the value of sin 30° + cos 60°?

  • A. 1/2
  • B. 1
  • C. √3/2
  • D. 2
Answer: B. 1
Explanation: sin 30° = 0.5. cos 60° = 0.5. 0.5 + 0.5 = 1.

Question 15

The radius of a circle is 7cm. Calculate its area. (Take π = 22/7)

  • A. 44 cm²
  • B. 88 cm²
  • C. 154 cm²
  • D. 49 cm²
Answer: C. 154 cm²
Explanation: Area = πr² = (22/7) x 7 x 7 = 154.

Question 16

Solve for x: 2 raised to the power (x - 1) = 16

  • A. 3
  • B. 4
  • C. 5
  • D. 6
Answer: C. 5
Explanation: 2^(x - 1) = 2^4. Therefore, x - 1 = 4. x = 5.

Question 17

Solve for x if 3x plus 5 equals 20.

  • A. 3
  • B. 5
  • C. 15
  • D. 25
Answer: B. 5
Explanation: Subtract 5 from 20 to get 15. Then divide 15 by 3 to get 5.

Question 18

Find the mean of the following set of numbers: 4, 8, 12, and 16.

  • A. 8
  • B. 10
  • C. 12
  • D. 40
Answer: B. 10
Explanation: Add the numbers (4 plus 8 plus 12 plus 16 equals 40). Divide the sum by the total count (40 divided by 4 equals 10).

Question 19

Convert the number 101 in base two to a number in base ten.

  • A. 3
  • B. 5
  • C. 6
  • D. 10
Answer: B. 5
Explanation: Calculation is (1 times 2 squared) plus (0 times 2 to the power of 1) plus (1 times 2 to the power of 0), which is 4 plus 0 plus 1 equals 5.

Question 20

A bag contains 3 red balls and 7 blue balls. What is the probability of picking a red ball at random?

  • A. 3 over 7
  • B. 3 over 10
  • C. 7 over 10
  • D. 1 over 3
Answer: B. 3 over 10
Explanation: Probability is the number of favorable outcomes (3 red balls) divided by the total number of outcomes (3 plus 7 equals 10).

Question 21

Simplify the expression: 2a plus 3b minus a plus 2b.

  • A. a plus b
  • B. 3a plus 5b
  • C. a plus 5b
  • D. 2a plus 5b
Answer: C. a plus 5b
Explanation: Group like terms: (2a minus a) plus (3b plus 2b) equals a plus 5b.

Question 22

If the sum of angles on a straight line is 180 degrees, find y if one angle is 70 degrees and the other is y.

  • A. 70 degrees
  • B. 90 degrees
  • C. 110 degrees
  • D. 180 degrees
Answer: C. 110 degrees
Explanation: Subtract the known angle from 180: (180 minus 70 equals 110).

Question 23

What is the value of 5 squared plus 2 cubed?

  • A. 17
  • B. 25
  • C. 33
  • D. 40
Answer: C. 33
Explanation: 5 squared is 25 and 2 cubed is 8. Add them together: (25 plus 8 equals 33).

Question 24

Calculate the simple interest on 2000 naira at a rate of 5 percent per annum for 2 years.

  • A. 100 naira
  • B. 200 naira
  • C. 400 naira
  • D. 500 naira
Answer: B. 200 naira
Explanation: Interest equals (Principal times Rate times Time) divided by 100. (2000 times 5 times 2) divided by 100 equals 200.

Question 25

Solve the inequality: 2x is greater than 10.

  • A. x is greater than 5
  • B. x is less than 5
  • C. x is greater than 20
  • D. x is equal to 5
Answer: A. x is greater than 5
Explanation: Divide both sides of the inequality by 2. 10 divided by 2 equals 5.

Question 26

Find the area of a rectangle with length 10cm and width 6cm.

  • A. 16cm squared
  • B. 32cm squared
  • C. 60cm squared
  • D. 100cm squared
Answer: C. 60cm squared
Explanation: Area of a rectangle equals length times width. (10 times 6 equals 60).

Question 27

What is the square root of 144?

  • A. 10
  • B. 12
  • C. 14
  • D. 16
Answer: B. 12
Explanation: 12 times 12 equals 144.

Question 28

If y is directly proportional to x, and y equals 10 when x equals 2, find y when x equals 5.

  • A. 15
  • B. 20
  • C. 25
  • D. 50
Answer: C. 25
Explanation: The constant of proportionality k is (y divided by x), which is 10 divided by 2 equals 5. When x is 5, y equals (5 times 5) which is 25.

Question 29

Find the value of x in the equation: x divided by 4 equals 8.

  • A. 2
  • B. 12
  • C. 32
  • D. 40
Answer: C. 32
Explanation: Multiply both sides by 4. (8 times 4 equals 32).

Question 30

A triangle has base 8cm and height 5cm. Calculate its area.

  • A. 13cm squared
  • B. 20cm squared
  • C. 40cm squared
  • D. 80cm squared
Answer: B. 20cm squared
Explanation: Area of a triangle equals (half times base times height). (0.5 times 8 times 5) equals 20.

Question 31

Express 0.00045 in standard form.

  • A. 4.5 times 10 to the power of negative 4
  • B. 4.5 times 10 to the power of negative 3
  • C. 45 times 10 to the power of negative 5
  • D. 0.45 times 10 to the power of negative 3
Answer: A. 4.5 times 10 to the power of negative 4
Explanation: Move the decimal point 4 places to the right to get 4.5. Since it moved right, the power is negative 4.

Question 32

Find the median of the following numbers: 3, 7, 9, 4, 6.

  • A. 4
  • B. 6
  • C. 7
  • D. 9
Answer: B. 6
Explanation: First, arrange in ascending order: 3, 4, 6, 7, 9. The middle number is 6.

Question 33

What is the sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral?

  • A. 180 degrees
  • B. 270 degrees
  • C. 360 degrees
  • D. 540 degrees
Answer: C. 360 degrees
Explanation: Any four sided figure has interior angles that sum up to 360 degrees.

Question 34

If 20 percent of a number is 40, what is the number?

  • A. 80
  • B. 100
  • C. 200
  • D. 800
Answer: C. 200
Explanation: If 20 percent is 40, then 100 percent is (40 divided by 20) times 100, which equals 200.

Question 35

The radius of a circle is 7cm. Find the circumference using pi as 22 over 7.

  • A. 22cm
  • B. 44cm
  • C. 88cm
  • D. 154cm
Answer: B. 44cm
Explanation: Circumference equals 2 times pi times radius. (2 times 22 over 7 times 7) equals 44.

Question 36

Solve for m: 4m minus 8 equals 0.

  • A. 2
  • B. 4
  • C. 8
  • D. 12
Answer: A. 2
Explanation: Add 8 to both sides to get 4m equals 8. Divide by 4 to get m equals 2.

Question 37

Find the LCM of 12 and 15.

  • A. 3
  • B. 30
  • C. 60
  • D. 180
Answer: C. 60
Explanation: Multiples of 12 are 12, 24, 36, 48, 60. Multiples of 15 are 15, 30, 45, 60. The lowest common multiple is 60.

Question 38

A car travels 120km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?

  • A. 60km per hour
  • B. 80km per hour
  • C. 120km per hour
  • D. 240km per hour
Answer: A. 60km per hour
Explanation: Speed equals distance divided by time. (120 divided by 2 equals 60).

Question 39

Evaluate: (3 plus 4) times (10 minus 2).

  • A. 15
  • B. 24
  • C. 56
  • D. 70
Answer: C. 56
Explanation: Solve the brackets first: 7 times 8 equals 56.

Question 40

If the diameter of a circle is 14cm, what is its radius?

  • A. 7cm
  • B. 14cm
  • C. 28cm
  • D. 44cm
Answer: A. 7cm
Explanation: Radius is half of the diameter. (14 divided by 2 equals 7).

Question 41

What is the value of 10 to the power of 0?

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. 10
  • D. 100
Answer: B. 1
Explanation: Any non zero number raised to the power of zero is always 1.

Question 42

Solve the simultaneous equations: x plus y equals 5 and x minus y equals 1. Find x.

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 5
Answer: B. 3
Explanation: Add the two equations: (x plus y) plus (x minus y) equals (5 plus 1). 2x equals 6, so x equals 3.

Question 43

Find the HCF of 24 and 36.

  • A. 6
  • B. 8
  • C. 12
  • D. 24
Answer: C. 12
Explanation: Factors of 24 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24. Factors of 36 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36. The highest common factor is 12.

Question 44

What is the gradient of the line y equals 3x plus 2?

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. x
  • D. 5
Answer: B. 3
Explanation: In the linear equation form y equals mx plus c, m is the gradient. Here m is 3.

Question 45

Find the third angle of a triangle if the other two angles are 50 degrees and 60 degrees.

  • A. 50 degrees
  • B. 60 degrees
  • C. 70 degrees
  • D. 110 degrees
Answer: C. 70 degrees
Explanation: Sum of angles in a triangle is 180. (180 minus 50 minus 60) equals 70.

Convert 0.75 to a fraction in its simplest form.

  • A. 1 over 2

  • C. 3 over 5
  • D. 75 over 100
Answer: B. 3 over 4
Explanation: 0.75 is 75 over 100. Dividing both by 25 gives 3 over 4.

The range of the set of numbers 2, 10, 15, 20, 25 is

  • A. 2
  • B. 20
  • C. 23
  • D. 25
Answer: C. 23
Explanation: Range is the difference between the highest and lowest values. (25 minus 2 equals 23).

How many sides does a hexagon have?

Evaluate the square root of 0.04.

  • A. 0.02

Question 50

In a right angled triangle, what is the value of the hypotenuse if the other two sides are 3cm and 4cm?

  • A. 5cm
  • B. 7cm
  • C. 12cm
  • D. 25cm
Answer: A. 5cm
Explanation: Using Pythagoras theorem: (3 squared plus 4 squared) equals (9 plus 16) which is 25. The square root of 25 is 5.
