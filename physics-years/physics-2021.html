Physics Past Questions 2025

Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

Which of the following is a supplementary unit in the S.I. system?

  • A. Kilogram
  • B. Radian
  • C. Candela
  • D. Mole
Answer: B. Radian
Explanation: Radian (for plane angles) and Steradian (for solid angles) are the two supplementary units.

Question 2

A car of mass 1200kg moving at 10 m/s is brought to rest in 20 seconds by a constant braking force. Calculate the magnitude of the force.

  • A. 600 N
  • B. 120 N
  • C. 2400 N
  • D. 60 N
Answer: A. 600 N
Explanation: Force = (mass * change in velocity) divided by time. F = (1200 * 10) / 20 = 12000 / 20 = 600 N.

Question 3

The area under a velocity-time graph represents:

  • A. Acceleration
  • B. Average speed
  • C. Distance travelled
  • D. Impulse
Answer: C. Distance travelled
Explanation: For a v-t graph, the area of the shape formed between the line and the time axis equals the total distance.

Question 4

Calculate the work done when a force of 20N stretches a wire by 0.4m.

  • A. 8.0 J
  • B. 4.0 J
  • C. 50.0 J
  • D. 16.0 J
Answer: B. 4.0 J
Explanation: Work done in stretching an elastic material = 0.5 * Force * Extension. W = 0.5 * 20 * 0.4 = 4 Joules.

Question 5

A body moves in a circle of radius 2.0m with a constant linear speed of 10 m/s. Calculate its angular velocity.

  • A. 20.0 rad/s
  • B. 5.0 rad/s
  • C. 0.2 rad/s
  • D. 2.0 rad/s
Answer: B. 5.0 rad/s
Explanation: Angular velocity (omega) = v / r. Omega = 10 / 2 = 5 rad/s.

Question 6

Which of the following is the dimension for Work?

  • A. MLT^-2
  • B. ML^2T^-2
  • C. ML^2T^-3
  • D. MLT^-1
Answer: B. ML^2T^-2
Explanation: Work = Force * Distance = (MLT^-2) * L = ML^2T^-2.

Question 7

The escape velocity from the Earth's surface is approximately:

  • A. 1.1 km/s
  • B. 11.2 km/s
  • C. 9.8 km/s
  • D. 11.2 m/s
Answer: B. 11.2 km/s
Explanation: This is the minimum speed needed for an object to break free from Earth's gravity without further propulsion.

Question 8

A machine has a mechanical advantage of 4 and an efficiency of 75 percent. Calculate its velocity ratio.

  • A. 3.00
  • B. 5.33
  • C. 300
  • D. 0.03
Answer: B. 5.33
Explanation: Efficiency = (MA / VR) * 100. 75 = (4 / VR) * 100. VR = 400 / 75 = 5.33.

Question 9

The handle of a door is usually placed as far as possible from the hinges to provide a:

  • A. Larger force
  • B. Smaller moment
  • C. Larger moment
  • D. Smaller friction
Answer: C. Larger moment
Explanation: Moment = Force * perpendicular distance. Increasing the distance makes it easier to turn the door with less effort.

Question 10

A bullet of mass 0.02kg is fired from a gun of mass 4kg. If the bullet leaves with a velocity of 200 m/s, calculate the recoil velocity of the gun.

  • A. 1.0 m/s
  • B. 0.1 m/s
  • C. 4.0 m/s
  • D. 10.0 m/s
Answer: A. 1.0 m/s
Explanation: (Mass of gun * Recoil velocity) = (Mass of bullet * Bullet velocity). 4 * V = 0.02 * 200. 4V = 4. V = 1.0 m/s.

Question 11

Which of the following materials is most elastic?

  • A. Rubber
  • B. Steel
  • C. Glass
  • D. Plasticine
Answer: B. Steel
Explanation: In physics, elasticity is measured by the ability to return to original shape after high stress. Steel has a much higher Young's Modulus than rubber.

Question 12

Upthrust on a body fully immersed in a liquid depends on the:

  • A. Weight of the body
  • B. Density of the body
  • C. Density of the liquid
  • D. Mass of the body
Answer: C. Density of the liquid
Explanation: Upthrust = Volume of liquid displaced * density of liquid * g.

Question 13

The pressure at a depth of 20m in water is P. What is the pressure at a depth of 20m in a liquid whose density is twice that of water?

  • A. P
  • B. 2P
  • C. 0.5P
  • D. 4P
Answer: B. 2P
Explanation: Pressure P = h * rho * g. If density (rho) doubles, pressure doubles for the same depth.

Question 14

The anomalous expansion of water occurs between:

  • A. -4 and 0 degrees C
  • B. 0 and 4 degrees C
  • C. 4 and 100 degrees C
  • D. 0 and 100 degrees C
Answer: B. 0 and 4 degrees C
Explanation: In this range, water contracts when heated and expands when cooled.

Question 15

Which of the following thermometers is used to measure very high temperatures, such as in a furnace?

  • A. Clinical thermometer
  • B. Alcohol thermometer
  • C. Optical pyrometer
  • D. Mercury-in-glass thermometer
Answer: C. Optical pyrometer
Explanation: Pyrometers measure heat by the intensity of light/radiation emitted by the hot object.

Question 16

The heat required to change the state of 1kg of a substance without a change in temperature is:

  • A. Specific heat capacity
  • B. Latent heat
  • C. Heat capacity
  • D. Internal energy
Answer: B. Latent heat
Explanation: "Latent" means hidden. This energy is used to break bonds during a phase change.

Question 17

A gas occupies 500 cm^3 at 27 degrees C. At what temperature will it occupy 1000 cm^3 at constant pressure?

  • A. 54 degrees C
  • B. 600 degrees C
  • C. 327 degrees C
  • D. 127 degrees C
Answer: C. 327 degrees C
Explanation: Using V1/T1 = V2/T2. Convert to Kelvin: T1 = 300K. 500/300 = 1000/T2. T2 = 600K. 600K - 273 = 327 degrees C.

Question 18

The process of heat transfer in a vacuum is:

  • A. Conduction
  • B. Convection
  • C. Radiation
  • D. Evaporation
Answer: C. Radiation
Explanation: Radiation does not require a material medium.

Question 19

Specific heat capacity is measured in:

  • A. J/kg
  • B. J/K
  • C. J/kg/K
  • D. kg/J/K
Answer: C. J/kg/K
Explanation: c = Q / (m * delta theta). Unit = Joule / (kg * Kelvin).

Question 20

The smell of perfume spreading across a room is an evidence of:

  • A. Osmosis
  • B. Diffusion
  • C. Brownian motion
  • D. Capillarity
Answer: B. Diffusion
Explanation: Diffusion is the movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to lower concentration.

Question 21

Which of the following waves requires a material medium for propagation?

  • A. Light waves
  • B. Radio waves
  • C. Sound waves
  • D. X-rays
Answer: C. Sound waves
Explanation: Sound is a mechanical wave and cannot travel through a vacuum.

Question 22

The image formed by a plane mirror is always:

  • A. Real, upright and same size
  • B. Virtual, inverted and same size
  • C. Virtual, upright and same size
  • D. Real, inverted and diminished
Answer: C. Virtual, upright and same size
Explanation: Plane mirror images cannot be caught on a screen (virtual) and are laterally inverted but upright.

Question 23

Calculate the refractive index of a medium if the speed of light in it is 2.0 x 10^8 m/s. (Speed of light in vacuum = 3.0 x 10^8 m/s)

  • A. 0.67
  • B. 1.50
  • C. 1.33
  • D. 2.00
Answer: B. 1.50
Explanation: Refractive index n = c / v. n = 3.0 / 2.0 = 1.5.

Question 24

The defect of the eye where a person sees far objects clearly but cannot see near objects is:

  • A. Myopia
  • B. Hypermetropia
  • C. Astigmatism
  • D. Cataract
Answer: B. Hypermetropia
Explanation: Also known as long-sightedness. It is corrected with a convex (converging) lens.

Question 25

Which of the following is a stringed instrument?

  • A. Flute
  • B. Drum
  • C. Violin
  • D. Trumpet
Answer: C. Violin
Explanation: Stringed instruments produce sound through the vibration of stretched strings.

Question 26

The loudness of sound is most closely related to its:

  • A. Frequency
  • B. Amplitude
  • C. Wavelength
  • D. Pitch
Answer: B. Amplitude
Explanation: Larger amplitude waves carry more energy and sound louder.

Question 27

The fundamental frequency of a closed pipe of length L is:

  • A. v / 2L
  • B. v / 4L
  • C. 2v / L
  • D. v / L
Answer: B. v / 4L
Explanation: In a closed pipe, the wavelength of the fundamental mode is 4 times the length of the pipe.

Question 28

When light passes from air into water, its frequency:

  • A. Increases
  • B. Decreases
  • C. Remains constant
  • D. Becomes zero
Answer: C. Remains constant
Explanation: During refraction, the speed and wavelength change, but the frequency depends only on the source and stays the same.

Question 29

A convex lens has a focal length of 10cm. Calculate its power.

  • A. 0.1 D
  • B. 10.0 D
  • C. 1.0 D
  • D. 5.0 D
Answer: B. 10.0 D
Explanation: Power P = 1 / f (in meters). f = 0.1m. P = 1 / 0.1 = 10 Dioptres.

Question 30

The color of light with the shortest wavelength in the visible spectrum is:

  • A. Red
  • B. Yellow
  • C. Blue
  • D. Violet
Answer: D. Violet
Explanation: Violet has the highest frequency and shortest wavelength. Red has the longest.

Question 31

The unit of electric potential difference is the:

  • A. Ampere
  • B. Ohm
  • C. Volt
  • D. Coulomb
Answer: C. Volt
Explanation: Volt is defined as one Joule per Coulomb.

Question 32

Three 4-ohm resistors are connected in series. What is the effective resistance?

  • A. 1.33 ohms
  • B. 4.00 ohms
  • C. 12.00 ohms
  • D. 0.75 ohm
Answer: C. 12.00 ohms
Explanation: For series connection, R = R1 + R2 + R3 = 4 + 4 + 4 = 12 ohms.

Question 33

A device used to store electric charge is a:

  • A. Resistor
  • B. Inductor
  • C. Capacitor
  • D. Diode
Answer: C. Capacitor
Explanation: Capacitors store energy in an electric field between two plates.

Question 34

The resistance of a conductor is 10 ohms. If a potential difference of 20V is applied, calculate the current.

  • A. 200 A
  • B. 0.5 A
  • C. 2.0 A
  • D. 10.0 A
Answer: C. 2.0 A
Explanation: I = V / R. I = 20 / 10 = 2 Amperes.

Question 35

The part of an AC generator that conducts current to the external circuit is the:

  • A. Commutator
  • B. Slip rings and brushes
  • C. Armature
  • D. Field magnet
Answer: B. Slip rings and brushes
Explanation: Slip rings allow the coil to rotate while maintaining electrical contact via the brushes.

Question 36

The magnetic field lines around a straight current-carrying wire are:

  • A. Straight lines parallel to the wire
  • B. Concentric circles
  • C. Radial lines
  • D. Elliptical loops
Answer: B. Concentric circles
Explanation: The direction is found using the Right-Hand Grip Rule.

Question 37

A step-up transformer has a primary coil of 100 turns and a secondary coil of 500 turns. If the input voltage is 20V, find the output voltage.

  • A. 100 V
  • B. 4 V
  • C. 200 V
  • D. 50 V
Answer: A. 100 V
Explanation: Vs / Vp = Ns / Np. Vs / 20 = 500 / 100. Vs = 20 * 5 = 100 Volts.

Question 38

Which of the following is an example of a "hard" magnetic material?

  • A. Soft iron
  • B. Steel
  • C. Nickel
  • D. Copper
Answer: B. Steel
Explanation: Hard magnetic materials are difficult to magnetize but retain their magnetism for a long time.

Question 39

The process of adding an impurity to a semiconductor to increase its conductivity is:

  • A. Doping
  • B. Rectification
  • C. Ionization
  • D. Polarization
Answer: A. Doping
Explanation: Doping creates n-type or p-type semiconductors.

Question 40

A semiconductor diode conducts current when it is:

  • A. Reverse biased
  • B. Forward biased
  • C. Not biased
  • D. Overheated
Answer: B. Forward biased
Explanation: Forward biasing reduces the depletion layer width, allowing current to flow.

Question 41

Cathode rays consist of fast-moving:

  • A. Protons
  • B. Neutrons
  • C. Electrons
  • D. Photons
Answer: C. Electrons
Explanation: Cathode rays are streams of electrons observed in vacuum tubes.

Question 42

The particle emitted in a radioactive decay that has a negative charge is the:

  • A. Alpha particle
  • B. Beta particle
  • C. Gamma ray
  • D. Positron
Answer: B. Beta particle
Explanation: A beta-minus particle is an electron.

Question 43

A radioactive substance has a half-life of 10 minutes. If 100g is present initially, how much remains after 30 minutes?

  • A. 50.0 g
  • B. 25.0 g
  • C. 12.5 g
  • D. 6.25 g
Answer: C. 12.5 g
Explanation: 30 minutes = 3 half-lives. Mass = 100 / (2^3) = 100 / 8 = 12.5g.

Question 44

Which radiation has the least ionizing power but the highest penetrating power?

  • A. Alpha particles
  • B. Beta particles
  • C. Gamma rays
  • D. X-rays
Answer: C. Gamma rays
Explanation: Gamma rays have no charge or mass, allowing them to pass through materials easily but interact less with atoms.

Question 45

The process of splitting a heavy nucleus into two smaller nuclei is:

  • A. Nuclear fusion
  • B. Nuclear fission
  • C. Radioactive decay
  • D. Thermionic emission
Answer: B. Nuclear fission
Explanation: This is the principle behind nuclear power plants and atomic bombs.

Question 46

Which of the following is a primary source of energy in the sun?

  • A. Fission of Uranium
  • B. Fusion of Hydrogen
  • C. Combustion of coal
  • D. Fission of Helium
Answer: B. Fusion of Hydrogen
Explanation: Hydrogen nuclei fuse to form Helium, releasing massive energy.

Question 47

Calculate the energy of a photon of light with a frequency of 5.0 x 10^14 Hz. (h = 6.6 x 10^-34 Js)

  • A. 3.3 x 10^-19 J
  • B. 1.3 x 10^-19 J
  • C. 3.3 x 10^48 J
  • D. 3.3 x 10^-48 J
Answer: A. 3.3 x 10^-19 J
Explanation: E = h * f. E = (6.6 x 10^-34) * (5.0 x 10^14) = 33 x 10^-20 = 3.3 x 10^-19 Joules.

Question 48

A p-type semiconductor is obtained by doping a pure semiconductor with a ______ element.

  • A. Group 2
  • B. Group 3
  • C. Group 4
  • D. Group 5
Answer: B. Group 3
Explanation: Group 3 elements (trivalent) create "holes" which act as positive charge carriers.

Question 49

The stopping potential in a photoelectric experiment depends on the:

  • A. Intensity of light
  • B. Surface area of the metal
  • C. Frequency of light
  • D. Time of exposure
Answer: C. Frequency of light
Explanation: Stopping potential is proportional to the maximum kinetic energy of the electrons, which is determined by frequency.

Question 50

The time taken for a satellite to complete one orbit around the Earth is its:

  • A. Velocity
  • B. Period
  • C. Acceleration
  • D. Range
Answer: B. Period
Explanation: Period is the time for one full revolution or cycle.
