Sample WAEC-style Practice Questions (Original Content)

WAEC Style

Question 1

Which of the following elements has the highest first ionization energy?

  • A. Sodium
  • B. Magnesium
  • C. Aluminum
  • D. Argon
Answer: D. Argon
Explanation: Ionization energy increases across a period. Argon, being a noble gas with a stable octet, requires the most energy to remove an electron.

Question 2

The type of bond formed between two non-metal atoms of different electronegativities is a:

  • A. Non-polar covalent bond
  • B. Polar covalent bond
  • C. Ionic bond
  • D. Metallic bond
Answer: B. Polar covalent bond
Explanation: When electronegativities differ, the shared electron pair is pulled closer to the more electronegative atom, creating poles.

Question 3

How many protons, neutrons, and electrons are in an atom of 23 Na 11?

  • A. 11 protons, 12 neutrons, 11 electrons
  • B. 11 protons, 11 neutrons, 12 electrons
  • C. 12 protons, 11 neutrons, 11 electrons
  • D. 11 protons, 23 neutrons, 11 electrons
Answer: A. 11 protons, 12 neutrons, 11 electrons
Explanation: Atomic number (11) = protons and electrons. Neutrons = Mass number (23) - Atomic number (11) = 12.

Question 4

Which of the following is an intensive property of matter?

  • A. Mass
  • B. Volume
  • C. Density
  • D. Length
Answer: C. Density
Explanation: Intensive properties do not depend on the amount of matter present. Density remains the same regardless of sample size.

Question 5

The geometric shape of a methane (CH4) molecule is:

  • A. Linear
  • B. Trigonal planar
  • C. Tetrahedral
  • D. Octahedral
Answer: C. Tetrahedral
Explanation: Methane has four bonding pairs and zero lone pairs, resulting in a tetrahedral shape with 109.5 degree angles.

Question 6

Which of the following gases is used as a refrigerant?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Ammonia
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Hydrogen
Answer: B. Ammonia
Explanation: Ammonia is widely used in industrial refrigeration because of its high latent heat of vaporization.

Question 7

The empirical formula of a compound containing 40% Carbon, 6.7% Hydrogen, and 53.3% Oxygen is: (C=12, H=1, O=16)

  • A. CHO
  • B. CH2O
  • C. C2H4O
  • D. C2H2O2
Answer: B. CH2O
Explanation: Moles: C=40/12=3.33; H=6.7/1=6.7; O=53.3/16=3.33. Ratio: 1:2:1.

Question 8

Which of the following salts is insoluble in water?

  • A. Sodium trioxocarbonate (IV)
  • B. Potassium chloride
  • C. Barium tetraoxosulfate (VI)
  • D. Calcium hydrogen trioxocarbonate (IV)
Answer: C. Barium tetraoxosulfate (VI)
Explanation: Most sulfates are soluble, but Barium, Calcium, and Lead sulfates are notable exceptions.

Question 9

The change of a gas to a liquid is called:

  • A. Evaporation
  • B. Sublimation
  • C. Condensation
  • D. Freezing
Answer: C. Condensation
Explanation: Condensation is the process where a gas loses heat and changes into the liquid state.

Question 10

What is the oxidation state of Manganese in KMnO4?

  • A. +2
  • B. +4
  • C. +6
  • D. +7
Answer: D. +7
Explanation: K(+1) + Mn + 4(-2) = 0. 1 + Mn - 8 = 0. Mn = +7.

Question 11

A gas occupies 2.0 dm3 at 27 degrees Celsius. What is its volume at 127 degrees Celsius at constant pressure?

  • A. 2.67 dm3
  • B. 3.0 dm3
  • C. 1.5 dm3
  • D. 4.0 dm3
Answer: A. 2.67 dm3
Explanation: Use Charles's Law (V1/T1 = V2/T2). Convert to Kelvin: T1=300K, T2=400K. 2.0/300 = V2/400. V2 = 2.67.

Question 12

Which of the following is the chief ore of Iron?

  • A. Bauxite
  • B. Haematite
  • C. Cassiterite
  • D. Galena
Answer: B. Haematite
Explanation: Haematite (Fe2O3) is the primary ore used in the blast furnace to extract iron.

Question 13

Which of the following is a greenhouse gas?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Helium
  • C. Methane
  • D. Argon
Answer: C. Methane
Explanation: Methane (CH4) is a potent greenhouse gas that contributes to global warming.

Question 14

The process of breaking down large hydrocarbon molecules into smaller ones is:

  • A. Polymerization
  • B. Cracking
  • C. Isomerization
  • D. Substitution
Answer: B. Cracking
Explanation: Cracking is used in the petroleum industry to produce smaller, more useful molecules like petrol.

Question 15

In the reaction: H2 + Cl2 -> 2HCl, which substance is oxidized?

  • A. Hydrogen
  • B. Chlorine
  • C. Hydrogen chloride
  • D. None of the above
Answer: A. Hydrogen
Explanation: The oxidation state of Hydrogen increases from 0 to +1, meaning it lost electrons (oxidized).

Question 16

The general formula for the Alkyne series is:

  • A. CnH2n+2
  • B. CnH2n
  • C. CnH2n-2
  • D. CnHn
Answer: C. CnH2n-2
Explanation: Alkynes are unsaturated hydrocarbons containing a carbon-carbon triple bond.

Question 17

Which of the following solutions will turn blue litmus paper red?

  • A. Sodium hydroxide
  • B. Ammonia solution
  • C. Hydrochloric acid
  • D. Sodium chloride
Answer: C. Hydrochloric acid
Explanation: Acids turn blue litmus paper red.

Question 18

The bond formed between a metal and a non-metal is usually:

  • A. Covalent
  • B. Metallic
  • C. Ionic (Electrovalent)
  • D. Dative
Answer: C. Ionic (Electrovalent)
Explanation: This occurs when electrons are transferred from the metal atom to the non-metal atom.

Question 19

How many electrons are shared in a double covalent bond?

  • A. 2
  • B. 4
  • C. 6
  • D. 8
Answer: B. 4
Explanation: A double bond consists of two pairs of shared electrons, making 4 electrons total.

Question 20

Which of the following is a neutralization reaction?

  • A. Acid + Metal -> Salt + Hydrogen
  • B. Acid + Base -> Salt + Water
  • C. Acid + Carbonate -> Salt + Water + CO2
  • D. Salt + Salt -> New Salt + New Salt
Answer: B. Acid + Base -> Salt + Water
Explanation: This is the classic definition of a neutralization reaction.

Question 21

What is the IUPAC name for CH3CH2CH2OH?

  • A. Methanol
  • B. Ethanol
  • C. Propan-1-ol
  • D. Butanol
Answer: C. Propan-1-ol
Explanation: The chain has 3 carbon atoms (propane) and an alcohol group (-OH) on the first carbon.

Question 22

The process by which vegetable oil is converted to margarine is:

  • A. Oxidation
  • B. Saponification
  • C. Hydrogenation
  • D. Esterification
Answer: C. Hydrogenation
Explanation: Adding hydrogen to unsaturated fats in the presence of a nickel catalyst makes them solid.

Question 23

Which of the following is the most reactive metal?

  • A. Copper
  • B. Iron
  • C. Potassium
  • D. Gold
Answer: C. Potassium
Explanation: Potassium is at the top of the reactivity series among the options listed.

Question 24

The formula for the trioxonitrate (V) acid is:

  • A. HNO2
  • B. HNO3
  • C. H2NO3
  • D. NH3
Answer: B. HNO3
Explanation: This is Nitric acid, where Nitrogen has an oxidation state of +5.

Question 25

Which of the following is a member of the transition metal series?

  • A. Sodium
  • B. Calcium
  • C. Iron
  • D. Magnesium
Answer: C. Iron
Explanation: Transition metals are found in the d-block of the periodic table (Groups 3-12).

Question 26

Anhydrous Calcium Chloride is used as a:

  • A. Catalyst
  • B. Drying agent (desiccant)
  • C. Indicator
  • D. Reducing agent
Answer: B. Drying agent (desiccant)
Explanation: It is deliquescent and absorbs moisture from gases or organic liquids.

Question 27

Which of the following isotopes is used in dating archaeological artifacts?

  • A. Carbon-12
  • B. Carbon-14
  • C. Uranium-235
  • D. Iodine-131
Answer: B. Carbon-14
Explanation: Radiocarbon dating uses the decay of C-14 to estimate the age of once-living materials.

Question 28

What is the percentage by mass of Oxygen in Water (H2O)? (H=1, O=16)

  • A. 11.1%
  • B. 33.3%
  • C. 88.9%
  • D. 50%
Answer: C. 88.9%
Explanation: Molar mass = 18. %O = (16/18) * 100 = 88.88...%.

Question 29

The ability of an atom to attract electrons to itself in a chemical bond is:

  • A. Electron affinity
  • B. Electronegativity
  • C. Ionization energy
  • D. Electropositivity
Answer: B. Electronegativity
Explanation: Electronegativity is a measure of the tendency of an atom to attract a bonding pair of electrons.

Question 30

The substance used to test for the presence of Glucose in a lab is:

  • A. Benedict's solution
  • B. Iodine solution
  • C. Litmus solution
  • D. Phenolphthalein
Answer: A. Benedict's solution
Explanation: Benedict's (or Fehling's) solution turns from blue to brick-red in the presence of a reducing sugar like glucose.

Question 31

Which of the following allotropes of Carbon is a good conductor of electricity?

  • A. Diamond
  • B. Graphite
  • C. Coal
  • D. Coke
Answer: B. Graphite
Explanation: Graphite has delocalized electrons between its layers, allowing it to conduct electricity.

Question 32

The volume of 0.1 mol/dm3 HCl required to neutralize 25 cm3 of 0.2 mol/dm3 NaOH is:

  • A. 12.5 cm3
  • B. 25.0 cm3
  • C. 50.0 cm3
  • D. 75.0 cm3
Answer: C. 50.0 cm3
Explanation: Using C1V1 = C2V2. 0.1 * V1 = 0.2 * 25. V1 = 5 / 0.1 = 50.

Question 33

Which gas is produced at the anode during the electrolysis of acidified water?

  • A. Hydrogen
  • B. Oxygen
  • C. Chlorine
  • D. Sulfur dioxide
Answer: B. Oxygen
Explanation: Hydroxyl ions are discharged at the anode to produce oxygen gas and water.

Question 34

The functional group for organic acids is:

  • A. -OH
  • B. -CHO
  • C. -COOH
  • D. -CO-
Answer: C. -COOH
Explanation: The carboxyl group is characteristic of alkanoic acids.

Question 35

Compounds with the same molecular formula but different structural formulas are called:

  • A. Isotopes
  • B. Allotropes
  • C. Isomers
  • D. Homologues
Answer: C. Isomers
Explanation: Isomerism allows different arrangements of the same atoms, resulting in different properties.

Question 36

Which of the following is a natural polymer?

  • A. Nylon
  • B. Polyethene
  • C. Cellulose
  • D. PVC
Answer: C. Cellulose
Explanation: Cellulose is found in plant cell walls and is a polymer of glucose.

Question 37

The gas that turns acidified Potassium Tetraoxomanganate (VII) from purple to colorless is:

  • A. CO2
  • B. SO2
  • C. NH3
  • D. O2
Answer: B. SO2
Explanation: Sulfur dioxide is a strong reducing agent and reduces the Mn(VII) ion.

Question 38

What is the solubility of a salt if 5g dissolves in 20g of water at 25 degrees Celsius? (g/100g water)

  • A. 5g
  • B. 10g
  • C. 25g
  • D. 100g
Answer: C. 25g
Explanation: (5g / 20g water) = (X / 100g water). X = (5 * 100) / 20 = 25.

Question 39

The process of soap making is called:

  • A. Esterification
  • B. Hydrolysis
  • C. Saponification
  • D. Polymerization
Answer: C. Saponification
Explanation: It involves the alkaline hydrolysis of fats and oils to produce soap and glycerol.

Question 40

Which of the following is an amphoteric oxide?

  • A. Na2O
  • B. MgO
  • C. Al2O3
  • D. SO2
Answer: C. Al2O3
Explanation: Aluminum oxide reacts with both acids and bases.

Question 41

The acid present in sour milk is:

  • A. Ethanoic acid
  • B. Lactic acid
  • C. Citric acid
  • D. Methanoic acid
Answer: B. Lactic acid
Explanation: Lactic acid is produced by the bacterial fermentation of lactose in milk.

Question 42

Temporary hardness in water can be removed by:

  • A. Filtration
  • B. Boiling
  • C. Adding common salt
  • D. Distillation only
Answer: B. Boiling
Explanation: Boiling decomposes the dissolved calcium hydrogen trioxocarbonate (IV) into insoluble calcium trioxocarbonate (IV).

Question 43

The product formed when ethene reacts with bromine water is:

  • A. Bromoethane
  • B. 1,2-dibromoethane
  • C. 1,1-dibromoethane
  • D. Ethane
Answer: B. 1,2-dibromoethane
Explanation: This is an addition reaction across the double bond of the alkene.

Question 44

Which of the following is a vector quantity in chemistry?

  • A. Temperature
  • B. Reaction rate
  • C. Dipole moment
  • D. Concentration
Answer: C. Dipole moment
Explanation: Dipole moment has both magnitude and a specific direction (from positive to negative pole).

Question 45

The salt obtained from the reaction between aqueous ammonia and sulfuric acid is:

  • A. Ammonium sulfate
  • B. Ammonia sulfate
  • C. Ammonium sulfite
  • D. Nitrogen sulfate
Answer: A. Ammonium sulfate
Explanation: (NH4)2SO4 is formed through neutralization.

Question 46

Which of the following methods is used to separate a mixture of oil and water?

  • A. Sublimation
  • B. Evaporation
  • C. Separating funnel
  • D. Fractional distillation
Answer: C. Separating funnel
Explanation: Oil and water are immiscible liquids and form distinct layers based on density.

Question 47

According to the Kinetic Theory, the pressure of a gas is caused by:

  • A. Attractive forces between molecules
  • B. Collisions of molecules with the walls of the container
  • C. The weight of the molecules
  • D. The volume of the molecules
Answer: B. Collisions of molecules with the walls of the container
Explanation: Each collision exerts a tiny force, and the sum of these forces over an area is pressure.

Question 48

The monomer used to make PVC is:

  • A. Ethene
  • B. Vinyl chloride (Chloroethene)
  • C. Propene
  • D. Tetrafluoroethene
Answer: B. Vinyl chloride (Chloroethene)
Explanation: Polyvinyl chloride (PVC) is produced by the polymerization of chloroethene.

Question 49

Which of the following is a use of Chlorine?

  • A. Filling balloons
  • B. Sterilizing water
  • C. Manufacturing margarine
  • D. Filling light bulbs
Answer: B. Sterilizing water
Explanation: Chlorine kills bacteria and other pathogens in drinking water and swimming pools.

Question 50

The noble gas used in incandescent light bulbs to prevent the filament from burning out is:

  • A. Helium
  • B. Neon
  • C. Argon
  • D. Radon
Answer: C. Argon
Explanation: Argon provides an inert atmosphere that prevents the Tungsten filament from oxidizing.
